Can You Open a UK Revolut Account Without a UK Phone Number?

Nov 10, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Revolut asks only for a valid mobile number, but sources disagree on whether a UK account needs a UK number. What is confirmed, and what to do.

Revolut's public materials ask for "a valid mobile number and email" and do not say the number must be British. A UK account does, however, require you to live in the UK, and whether a given number is accepted depends on which country codes the app currently supports. Sources disagree, and we found no official statement guaranteeing that a US number can open a UK account.

What the official requirements say

  • According to Wise's summary of Revolut's requirements, a UK personal account requires you to be 18 or over and resident in the UK, with a valid mobile number and email, a valid government ID such as a passport plus a selfie, and your name, date of birth and nationality.
  • The same source notes that non-UK residents may be able to open an account with the Revolut entity for their own country, with different features, fees and terms.
  • Revolut's US sign-up guide requires you to be 18 or over, a lawful US resident, with a valid US mobile number, email, government ID and SSN. The US account is explicit about needing a US number.

The practical limit: the country-code list

  • At sign-up you pick your number's country code from a list in the app. Community posts report that some countries (Pakistan, Serbia and Ukraine were mentioned) either do not receive SMS or are missing from the list.
  • One community suggestion is to log out and check the supported codes on the login screen. Revolut's help centre has a page on supported countries; rely on its current version.
  • SMS verification is mandatory, so the number must be able to receive international texts.

If you live in the US

  1. Check whether you are actually UK-resident. A UK account requires UK residence. If you live in the US, the US Revolut account (US number and SSN) is usually the right product.
  2. Check the code list in the app for +1.
  3. Use a real number that receives SMS, one you will keep long term; avoid temporary numbers.
  4. Keep the number. It is used for login and verification, and recovery after losing it is slow.
  5. Ask support when unsure, through in-app chat, and keep a screenshot of the answer.

Pitfalls when changing number or moving

  • In one community case, a user who moved to the US typed a US number into an existing UK account, was locked out and told a new account had been created. Change the number inside the existing account first, then the country.
  • Another post reports a "number already in use by another account" error when re-registering with a foreign SIM.

Risks

  • Virtual numbers can trigger risk controls or cost you account access if codes stop arriving.
  • An address or country of residence that does not match reality can lead to rejection at KYC or later closure.

FAQ

Can a US number register a UK Revolut account?

Nothing official says no, and nothing guarantees yes. What matters is whether +1 appears in the code list and whether you are UK-resident. Check the app and support.

Can I use a virtual number?

Some sellers say so, but we found no official Revolut endorsement. Not advisable for a long-term account.

I moved to the US. How do I change the number on my UK account?

Update it inside the UK account first and complete verification. Do not re-register with the new number, which may create a second account or trigger a lock.
