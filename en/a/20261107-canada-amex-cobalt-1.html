Does the Amex Cobalt charge a foreign transaction fee?

Nov 7, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

The Cobalt card charges 2.5% on non-CAD purchases, and foreign spending earns only the base 1x. Costs, a worked example and alternatives.

Yes. Several Canadian card comparison sites, including Ratehub and Credit Card Genius, list a 2.5% foreign transaction fee on non-Canadian-dollar purchases with the American Express Cobalt Card in Canada, with no waiver. Foreign spending also earns only the base 1 point per dollar, so using this card abroad is usually a poor deal.

Note: we could not locate the fee schedule on Amex Canada's own site; the figures above come from third-party comparison sites. Check the Rates and Fees page on Amex Canada or in your account before you travel.

Points are affected too

Cobalt once earned elevated points on dining and groceries abroad, which some argued offset the fee. Credit Card Genius reports that Amex began enforcing existing terms: bonus rates apply only to purchases from Canadian merchants, and non-CAD spend earns 1 point per dollar. The article puts the return at roughly 2% at most, a net loss after the 2.5% fee.

Ratehub also notes that travel earn fell from 2x to 1x in October 2024. Milesopedia gives differing dates for the foreign earn restriction, from 2024 to 2025-26, but the conclusion is the same: no bonus points abroad.

The arithmetic

  • C$1,000 equivalent spent abroad: about C$25 in fees.
  • C$5,000: about C$125.
  • That excludes any spread in Amex's exchange rate, for which we found no authoritative figure.

At a common valuation of 1-2 cents per point, a 1x earn rate does not cover 2.5%.

Before you travel

  1. Check the card terms: sign in to Amex Canada and open Rates and Fees.
  2. Carry a no-foreign-fee card as your main card abroad.
  3. Pay in the local currency, not Canadian dollars, to avoid dynamic currency conversion.
  4. Keep Cobalt for domestic dining, groceries and subscriptions, where 5x and 3x matter.
  5. Check your statement on return to confirm the fees posted as expected.

FAQ

Does Cobalt charge 2.5% in the US too?

Any purchase not priced in Canadian dollars is a non-CAD purchase and, per third-party sources, incurs 2.5%. If a merchant offers to bill in CAD, the cost may simply move into the exchange rate, usually at a worse price.

Is foreign spend always 1x?

According to Credit Card Genius, bonus rates apply only to Canadian merchants, so foreign spend generally earns 1 point per dollar. Amex's current terms govern.

Can the fee be waived?

Ratehub states there is no waiver, and we found no official route. Use a no-foreign-fee card abroad.
