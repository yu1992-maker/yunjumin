Capital One to Copa: Is the 2:1.5 Ratio Worth It With a 30% Transfer Bonus?

Nov 11, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Capital One added Copa ConnectMiles on 2 November: normally 2:1.5, with a 30% bonus until 1 December that works out to 1,000 to 975.

Capital One miles transfer to Copa ConnectMiles at a standard 1,000 : 750 (2:1.5). The partnership launched on 2 November 2026, and a 30% transfer bonus runs until 11:59pm ET on 1 December, making the effective rate 1,000 to 975. Whether to transfer depends on having a specific Copa or Star Alliance award to book. Without one, do not transfer just for the bonus.

The rules at a glance

ItemDetail
Launch date2 November 2026
Standard ratio1,000 Capital One miles → 750 ConnectMiles
Transfer bonus30%, ending 11:59pm ET on 1 December
Ratio with bonus1,000 → 975 (about 0.975:1)
Minimum transfer1,000 miles (per Frequent Miler); another source cites increments of 100

On ConnectMiles expiry, several reports say the miles expire after 24 months of inactivity, while Capital One miles generally do not expire while the account is open. We could not find Copa's official terms; check Copa's site before transferring.

Even with the bonus, Capital One's ratio is slightly worse than 1:1 from Citi premium cards or Rove. If you hold those points, look there first. Capital One's advantage is that many people already have large balances and need no new card.

What Copa miles can buy

  • Copa's own flights: economy within North America Zone 1 from 15,000 miles one way (View from the Wing).
  • United: premium transcontinental business class at 25,000 miles one way when saver space is available.
  • Emirates: US to Dubai in business class for 80,000 miles one way with no carrier surcharges, though taxes still apply; availability is required, and Frequent Miler notes it is hard to find.
  • Other partners: ANA, Lufthansa, Swiss, Air France and others. Frequent Miler says Copa passes on no surcharges for the first four.

One reader comment reported Lufthansa business class from Amsterdam at 90,000 miles per person, with about $500 in taxes for three seats plus an infant. This is a single unverified comment.

Four steps to decide

  1. Check availability first. Search your flight on Copa's site with a ConnectMiles account. No saver space, no transfer.
  2. Work out how many ConnectMiles you need. For 80,000 miles, you need about 82,100 Capital One miles during the bonus (80,000 ÷ 0.975); rounding to increments of 100 makes it slightly more. Confirm on the transfer page.
  3. Compare alternatives. What would the same flight cost via another Capital One partner, such as the operating airline's own program? If you have Citi or Rove points, compare again.
  4. Check taxes and expiry. How much cash in taxes? Can you use the miles within 24 months?

How to transfer

  1. Log in to Capital One and open the Transfer page.
  2. Choose Copa ConnectMiles and link your member number.
  3. Enter the amount and confirm the post-bonus figure shown.
  4. Submit before 11:59pm ET on 1 December to receive the bonus.

Transfers are generally irreversible, so confirm availability before submitting.

FAQ

1. With the 30% bonus, does it beat Citi?

No. At 1,000→975 it remains slightly below 1:1 from Citi premium cards and Rove. If you have neither, transferring during the bonus is still better than the standard 750.

2. Do the miles expire?

Reports say ConnectMiles expire after 24 months of inactivity; Capital One miles generally do not expire while the account is open. Transfer only with a booking in mind.

3. Will there be a bonus after 1 December?

The only bonus we found ends on 1 December. Nothing has been announced beyond that, and we will not speculate.

Information as of 11 November 2026. Transfer ratios, bonuses, award space and pricing can change at any time; verify with Capital One and Copa, and consult a professional for complex situations.
