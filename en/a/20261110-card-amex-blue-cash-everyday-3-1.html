Amex Blue Cash Everyday 3% Cash Back: Caps, Categories and Exclusions

Nov 10, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Blue Cash Everyday earns 3% at U.S. supermarkets, U.S. gas stations and U.S. online retail, each capped at $6,000 a year. Which merchants count, and which do not.

The Amex Blue Cash Everyday (BCE) has no annual fee. It earns 3% cash back in three categories: U.S. supermarkets, U.S. gas stations and U.S. online retail purchases, on the first $6,000 per category per calendar year, then 1%. With all three maxed, $18,000 of spending earns 3%, for at most $540. Everything else earns 1%.

The hard part is not the rate but which merchants qualify. Amex goes by the merchant category code the merchant reports, not by intuition.

Caps reset each calendar year (January 1 to December 31), not on your card anniversary. As of November 10, 2026, if one category is close to $6,000 for the year, shift that spending to another card until January.

Supermarkets: what counts and what does not

Amex defines supermarkets as traditional stores that mainly sell food. According to Amex terms compiled by Eye of the Flyer, these earn only 1%:

  • Superstores and supercenters (e.g. Walmart, Target)
  • Warehouse clubs (e.g. Costco, Sam's Club)
  • Convenience stores
  • Meal-kit delivery (e.g. Home Chef)
  • Fuel bought at supermarkets or warehouse clubs (also 1%)

Whether specialty Asian supermarkets code as supermarkets is not settled across sources; it depends on the merchant's code. A practical approach is to make a small first purchase and check whether 3% posts. Amex does not address Whole Foods or Amazon explicitly; test with a small amount as well.

Online retail: the easiest place to slip

Under the terms, online retail means U.S. retailers selling physical goods through a website or app, paid online.

Two points deserve separating:

  • Search-result summaries say most U.S. e-commerce sites such as Amazon, Walmart.com and Target.com earn 3%, while the same items bought in store earn 1%. That is a third-party summary, not an Amex merchant-by-merchant guarantee.
  • Amex terms expressly exclude: services (travel, entertainment), restaurants, supermarkets, gas stations, car dealers, phone or mail orders, online orders paid in store, in-store mobile tap payments, and third-party buy-now-pay-later.

So groceries ordered on Walmart.com may be treated as supermarket spend and miss 3%. Do not expect 3% on flights, hotels or subscriptions.

How to use it well

  1. Estimate annual spending in each category; anything above $6,000 earns 1%.
  2. Use the card for U.S. online purchases of physical goods.
  3. Buy groceries at traditional supermarket chains, not Costco or Walmart.
  4. Fill up at regular gas stations, not warehouse clubs.
  5. Switch cards as a category nears $6,000.
  6. Do not buy gift cards, prepaid cards or send person-to-person payments; these never earn and do not count toward bonus spend.

Fees and welcome offer

  • Annual fee: $0
  • Foreign transaction fee: 2.7%; do not use abroad
  • Balance transfer fee: the greater of $5 or 3%
  • Welcome offer (per Eye of the Flyer): up to $200 after $2,000 of spending in the first 6 months, posted 8–12 weeks after qualifying. Past holders of BCE, Blue Cash Preferred or Cash Magnet may be ineligible.
  • Enrollment credits: Home Chef up to $15 a month; Disney/Hulu/ESPN up to $7 a month

Offers change; rely on the Amex page on the day you apply.

Where sources disagree

One Finder guide shows both $2,000 and $6,000 as the cap on the same page. Amex and nearly every other source say $6,000 per category; we use $6,000 and treat Finder's figure as an error.

Clark.com's modelling suggests the Synchrony Premier World Mastercard may out-earn this card for some spending mixes unless your spending is concentrated in groceries, gas and online shopping. It depends on your own profile.

FAQ

Does Costco earn 3%?

No. Warehouse clubs are not supermarkets and earn 1%, as does fuel at warehouse clubs.

Is the 3% cap based on my account opening date or the year?

The calendar year. Each category is independent at $6,000, then 1%.

If I pay an online retailer with Apple Pay in store, is that online?

No. Amex terms say in-store mobile tap payments are not online retail. To earn 3%, the order must be for physical goods paid online through the merchant's website or app.
