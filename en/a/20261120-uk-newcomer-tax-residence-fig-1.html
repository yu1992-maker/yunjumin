New to the UK? Tax Residence, the £12,570 Allowance and the 4-Year Foreign Income Rule

Nov 20, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

2026/27 Personal Allowance £12,570 with 20/40/45% bands; 183+ days usually means UK resident; since April 2025 new arrivals can claim 4 years of relief on foreign income and gains (FIG).

after moving to the UK, first work out whether you're UK tax resident. If you are, your worldwide income is in principle taxable in the UK. But since 6 April 2025, new residents who were non-resident for at least 10 consecutive years can claim the 4-year foreign income and gains (FIG) regime, which replaced the old remittance basis.

Compiled from GOV.UK and HMRC (checked November 2026); the official pages prevail.

1. Are you UK tax resident?

The UK uses the Statutory Residence Test (SRT). GOV.UK's example: spending more than 183 days in the UK in a tax year (6 April – 5 April) makes you resident. You can also be resident with fewer days because of a UK home, work and other ties—complex cases need the full test.

2. 2026/27 rates and allowance (England, Wales, Northern Ireland)

The £12,570 allowance is frozen and tapers away once income exceeds £100,000. Scotland has its own six bands for earnings (19% to 48%).

3. Foreign income: the 4-year FIG regime

  • Who qualifies: you were non-UK resident for at least 10 consecutive tax years before becoming resident, and you can only claim in your first 4 years of residence.
  • How to claim: on your Self Assessment return—separately for each year and each source of foreign income; unused years can't be carried forward.
  • The cost: in any year you claim, you lose your Personal Allowance (£12,570) and the capital gains annual exempt amount. If your foreign income is small, claiming may leave you worse off—do the maths first.
  • Leaving mid-way: a year in which you're non-resident can't be claimed and doesn't roll forward.

4. Do you need to file?

If you only have UK salary taxed through PAYE, usually not. If you have foreign income taxable in the UK, or want to claim FIG, you need Self Assessment. Online returns for 2025/26 are due 31 January 2027; first-time filers had to register by 5 October 2026.

FAQ

Is interest or rent from my home country taxable in the UK?

Once UK resident, in principle it must be reported. If eligible you can claim the 4-year FIG relief, but weigh the loss of allowances. Tax already paid abroad may be creditable under a tax treaty—ask a professional.

How are the 183 days counted?

By days in the UK within the tax year (from 6 April); detailed rules (such as counting midnights) are in HMRC's SRT guidance.
