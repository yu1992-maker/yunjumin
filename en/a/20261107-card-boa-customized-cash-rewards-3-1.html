BofA Customized Cash Rewards: choosing the 3% category, changing it and the cap

Nov 7, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

The 3% category can be changed once per calendar month and shares a $2,500 quarterly cap with 2% grocery spending. How to change it, how to choose and what to avoid.

You choose the 3% category on Bank of America Customized Cash Rewards yourself and can change it once each calendar month, only through online banking or the mobile app. The chosen category's 3% (6% in the first year) and the 2% at grocery stores and wholesale clubs share a $2,500 quarterly cap; spending above it earns 1%. Changing categories does not reset the cap.

Available 3% categories

The card page currently lists:

  • Gas stations and EV charging stations
  • Online shopping (the bank includes cable, internet, phone plans and streaming here)
  • Dining
  • Travel
  • Drug stores
  • Home improvement and furnishings

Sources differ: earlier coverage (for example Doctor of Credit and NerdWallet on the category expansion) described cable, streaming, internet and phone as a separate category, reportedly for personal cards only. Category names and groupings change, so rely on what your online banking shows.

Some sources say the default, if you never choose, is gas and EV charging. That comes from search snippets; confirm in online banking.

How to change the category

  1. Sign in to Bank of America online banking or open the app.
  2. Open the card account and find the Choice Category option. Menu locations vary over time; searching the page for "category" helps.
  3. Select the new category and save.
  4. The change applies to subsequent purchases. Change it before a large purchase rather than hoping for a retroactive fix.

One change per calendar month: a change made at month-end can be followed by another on the 1st.

Choosing each quarter

  1. Start with the quarter's biggest expense. Summer flights and hotels suggest Travel; winter utility and internet bills suggest Online shopping.
  2. Bunch large purchases into one month. With one change a month, schedule foreseeable big purchases after the category is set.
  3. Know the ceiling. $2,500 × 3% = $75; $2,500 × 6% = $150 in year one. That is the most per quarter; beyond it you earn 1%.
  4. Limit grocery spending on this card. Groceries eat into the shared cap. If another card pays more on groceries, use it and save the cap for the 3% category.

Preferred Rewards boost

Bank of America says BofA Preferred Rewards members earn a bonus on card rewards. The card page does not detail the percentages or thresholds (typically tied to deposit and investment balances with the bank); some reviews say it can lift 3% to as much as 5.25%. Sources vary; confirm the exact figures with Bank of America.

FAQ

1. I changed the category this month and chose wrong. What now?

Under the official rule you get one change per calendar month, so you must wait until next month. Decide before you change.

2. Does changing the category reset the cap?

No. BestMoney's guide says changes do not reset or add to the cap, and Bank of America describes the cap as the first $2,500 combined per quarter. That BestMoney page contains placeholder text, so treat Bank of America's wording as authoritative.

3. Can I keep using the card after $2,500?

Yes, at 1%. At that point another card with a higher rate may be better.
