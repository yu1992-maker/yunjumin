Bank of America Fees Rise February 19, 2027: $15 Monthly, $15 Overdraft

Nov 24, 20266 min readYunjumin Forum editors

The three Advantage Plus waivers are unchanged; the old 75% card bonus protects existing cards only and lapses by anniversary month.

Under the Bank of America fee schedule effective November 13, 2026, three prices rise on February 19, 2027: the Advantage Plus monthly fee goes from $12 to $15, the overdraft item fee from $10 to $15, and the domestic non-network ATM fee from $2.50 to $3.00. The waivers are unchanged: one qualifying direct deposit of $250, a minimum daily balance of $1,500, or BofA Rewards status at Preferred Plus or above. Legacy members' card bonuses expire on a separate calendar, and the protection covers existing cards only.

This article draws on Bank of America's Personal Schedule of Fees, the BofA Rewards page and Merrill's program page. For the card-bonus transition we also cite Doctor of Credit, My Money Blog and Frequent Miler. Checked November 24, 2026. The schedule covers personal accounts in all states, not business accounts.

"Minimum daily balance" means the lowest point in the statement cycle, not the average. One day below $1,500 and the waiver fails.

2. Overdrafts: what never triggers the fee

The overdraft fee rises by half, lifting the daily ceiling from $20 to $30. The schedule also lists what is never charged: everyday one-time debit card purchases and ATM transactions; items authorized when funds were sufficient; items of $1 or less, or that overdraw the account by $1 or less; items declined or returned; and any account on the Decline All setting, even if the balance ends up negative.

What does trigger $15 is the check, the ACH debit, the recurring debit card charge and the automatic bill payment. The bank does not charge for a returned item, but the payee may.

3. Tier review and card bonus run on different calendars

The first calendar is the tier review. The schedule provides for an Annual Review in each enrollment-anniversary month (legacy Preferred Rewards members keep their original date), using a three-calendar-month rolling average. If you fall short you are notified and given a grace period of at least three months; only if the monthly checks, on the third business day, still fail are you moved down.

The second is the transitional card bonus. The official page says only that accounts of customers enrolled before May 26, 2026 "may be eligible for an extended card benefit" and directs them to sign in. Accounts of the exact date differ:

  • A banker memo quoted by Doctor of Credit: existing cards keep the old bonus until the first anniversary month on or after November 1, 2026, when the tier is re-evaluated, with the adjustment three months later. Its example: a Platinum Honors member with a July anniversary is reviewed in July 2027 and drops from 75% to 50% in October 2027.
  • A customer email quoted by My Money Blog: at least until the anniversary following November 2026, so April 2027 for an April anniversary, with no mention of three months.
  • The Bank of America alumni page: at least six months after launch.

On the memo's reading, a former Platinum Honors member who remains at Preferred Honors would see this timetable on existing cards:

On November 24, 2026 Doctor of Credit added the point most easily missed: the protection attaches to existing cards. A newly approved card is on the new scale at once, so a $100,000 member with a 1.5% card earns 2.25% on the new one rather than 2.625%, even while an older card still carries 75%.

4. Worked examples

A newcomer without direct deposit. With about $800 in Advantage Plus, the annual cost rises from $144 to $180. Keeping $1,500 there avoids it; if that money could earn a hypothetical 4%, the opportunity cost is $60 a year, still below $180. Someone who writes no checks can move to SafeBalance, keep $500, and pay no monthly or overdraft fee.

A former Gold member averaging $25,000. The alumni page maps Gold to Preferred Plus, but the threshold is now $30,000. If the average is still $25,000 after review and grace, the tier falls to Member: the card bonus goes from 25% to 10%, and the tier-based fee waiver ends. Advantage Plus remains free because the balance exceeds $1,500; what is lost is the waiver of the $15 incoming domestic and international wire fees.

A former Platinum Honors member spending $20,000 a year. A 1.5% card returns $525 at 75% and $450 at 50%, a loss of $75. Preferred Honors offers subscription credits of up to $8 a month, or $96 a year. If fully used they cover the gap; break-even is $25,600 of spending ($96 divided by 0.375%).

5. Pro tips

  1. The 30-day window at first enrollment. For the first 30 calendar days the tier is set on daily balances, which need to stay above the threshold for only three business days. The official page adds that a later dip does not cost you the tier before the next anniversary. Have the funds in place before you enroll. The window applies to first enrollment only.
  2. Switch account type, keep the number. Moving between SafeBalance, Advantage Plus and Advantage Relationship does not change the account number. SafeBalance lacks paper checks and Balance Connect, so check how you pay rent first.
  3. Investments count. Deposits, Merrill and Private Bank investment accounts, IRAs, and 529 plans on Merrill statements qualify. Employer 401(k)s, annuities, UTMA custodial accounts and business-owned accounts do not.
  4. Keep uncategorized spend on the old card. Members with anniversaries in late 2027 retain the higher rate there longest. A new card suits a welcome offer, not a long-term 2.625% strategy.
  5. Check the currency on outgoing wires. An international wire in U.S. dollars costs $45; one sent in foreign currency carries no wire fee but includes an exchange-rate markup. Compare total cost.

6. Common misconceptions

  • "The $1,500 threshold rises too." The schedule lists fee changes only; no change to waivers appears.
  • "Any transfer is a direct deposit." The schedule requires a qualifying direct deposit, defined in its FAQ section. Do not plan around Zelle or your own transfers.
  • "You drop a tier on the anniversary." A grace period of at least three months follows.
  • "The bonus applies to everything." Account-opening bonuses and non-standard promotional rewards are excluded unless stated otherwise.

FAQ

Where do I find my anniversary date?

According to Doctor of Credit, at the top right of the online banking home page, or in the program menu of the app. It is the enrollment date, not the account-opening date. For the extended card benefit, rely on what BofA Rewards shows after sign-in.

Does a product change preserve the old bonus?

Neither the official pages nor the sources above say. Get a clear answer from the bank first.

Does Member tier waive the monthly fee?

No. The tier-based waiver starts at Preferred Plus.
