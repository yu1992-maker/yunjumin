Selling Physical Goods in Florida: Does a New Mexico LLC Need a Florida Sales Tax Registration?

Nov 7, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

A New Mexico LLC selling goods locally in Florida generally must register for Florida sales tax; remote sellers depend on physical presence and the $100,000 threshold.

If you live in Florida, keep inventory in Florida and sell physical goods there, a New Mexico LLC generally must register for sales tax with the Florida Department of Revenue (FDOR). New Mexico is only where the company was formed and does not replace Florida registration. The one exception: you truly have no Florida physical presence and sell only through marketplaces such as Amazon or Etsy, which collect the tax for you.

Two separate obligations

  • Sales tax registration with the Florida Department of Revenue, via the Florida Business Tax Application or paper Form DR-1.
  • Foreign LLC qualification with the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz), Form CR2E027.

Different agencies, different duties. A sales tax number does not complete foreign qualification, and vice versa.

Note: the FDOR page we reviewed states that a marketplace seller with physical presence must register as a dealer and collect tax on Florida sales made outside the marketplace. For founders living in Florida, this is the key rule.

Why a New Mexico LLC does not avoid Florida tax

  • Sales tax follows where you operate and where you sell, not the state of formation.
  • A New Mexico LLC run by a Florida resident in Florida is doing business in Florida, which usually also raises foreign qualification.
  • Foreign qualification fees: several third-party sources cite $125 in total ($100 filing plus $25 registered agent designation), with a Certificate of Existence from the home state. Third-party figures; rely on Sunbiz.
  • Annual report: third-party sources cite $138.75 due by May 1; a $400 late fee appears in only one source and should be verified.

Registration steps

  1. Confirm the goods are taxable. Florida taxes most tangible goods, with exemptions for categories such as certain food and medicine; check FDOR guidance.
  2. File the Florida Business Tax Application online or mail Form DR-1. Official guidance lists no registration fee.
  3. Wait: the official page suggests checking status after three business days.
  4. Receive the welcome package by mail: Certificate of Registration, Annual Resale Certificate and new dealer guide.
  5. File every period on your assigned frequency, even when no tax is due.

Remote sellers: county surtax and past periods

  • Remote sellers over the threshold collect state tax plus any applicable county surtax.
  • Registering before October 1, 2021 waived tax, penalties and interest on remote sales before July 1, 2021; that window has closed.
  • Whether to keep collecting after sales fall below the threshold: one source says Florida has no statute or public FDOR guidance on this. A grey area; ask a CPA.

FAQ

My LLC is in New Mexico. Do I still need a Florida sales tax number?

Yes, if you have Florida physical presence or exceed $100,000 in remote sales. Sales tax is driven by where you operate and sell.

I sell only on Amazon. Must I register?

Marketplace sales where the platform certifies collection generally need nothing from you. But with Florida physical presence (living there, inventory there), FDOR requires registration and tax on off-platform sales. Confirm physical presence with FDOR or a CPA.

What if I started selling before registering?

FDOR says dealers must register before starting business. Late returns carry a 10% penalty (minimum $50) and unpaid tax accrues floating-rate interest. We found no specific penalty for non-registration; consult a professional.

Reminder: For reference only; rely on FDOR and Sunbiz. For FBA, multistate sales or foreign qualification, consult a CPA or attorney.
