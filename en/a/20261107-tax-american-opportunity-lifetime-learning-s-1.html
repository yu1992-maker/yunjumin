Education Credits: the New SSN Rule for AOTC and LLC in 2026

Nov 7, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

AOTC is worth up to $2,500 and the LLC $2,000. From tax year 2026 filer and student need a work-valid SSN; an ITIN is not enough.

Two main US credits cover tuition: the American Opportunity Tax Credit (AOTC), up to $2,500 per student, and the Lifetime Learning Credit (LLC), up to $2,000 per return, both claimed on Form 8863. The change is that from tax year 2026 (returns filed in early 2027) both require a Social Security number valid for employment. An ITIN alone no longer qualifies.

The IRS page lists only the income limits above. For the phase-out near the limit see the Form 8863 instructions or Publication 970; we have not verified those figures.

The new SSN rule for 2026

According to the IRS (EITC Central), claiming either credit for 2026 requires:

  • A valid SSN issued by the SSA for the filer, the spouse on a joint return, and a student claimed as a dependent.
  • Issue on or before the filing due date, including extensions.
  • An SSN valid for employment in the United States.
  • An SSN issued only to obtain a federally funded benefit such as Medicaid cannot be used.
  • People who previously claimed these credits with an ITIN are no longer eligible unless they meet the new requirement.

Common situations

  • Parent with an ITIN only, child with an SSN: not eligible (TaxAct example).
  • Parent with an SSN, child with an ITIN only: not eligible (same source).
  • Joint return, one spouse with an SSN and one with an ITIN, student with an SSN: TaxAct considers this eligible.

Sources differ: the IRS page says the spouse on a joint return must also have a valid SSN, while TaxAct says one is enough. Couples in this position should confirm with a CPA or the final Form 8863 instructions.

If the card says "Not valid for employment"

  1. Check whether the card reads "VALID FOR WORK ONLY WITH DHS AUTHORIZATION" or "NOT VALID FOR EMPLOYMENT".
  2. If you have since become a citizen or permanent resident, ask the SSA for an unrestricted card.
  3. Restrictions vary by case; ask a CPA if unsure.

Tax years before 2026 are outside the new rule and follow the old one.

How to claim

  1. Wait for Form 1098-T around the end of January. Box 1 may differ from what you paid, so reconcile it.
  2. If it should have arrived and has not, contact the school. Otherwise keep proof of enrolment and payment.
  3. Confirm the SSN status of filer, spouse (if joint) and student.
  4. Complete Form 8863 and attach it to Form 1040 or 1040-SR. The AOTC needs the school's EIN.
  5. For one student in one year choose either credit, not both for the same expenses.

FAQ

I hold an ITIN and claimed the AOTC before. Can I in 2026?

The IRS says earlier ITIN claimants no longer qualify unless they meet the new SSN requirement.

My child is a student with an ITIN only, and I have an SSN. Can I claim?

The IRS requires a valid SSN for a student claimed as a dependent, so usually not. International students filing for themselves should consult a CPA.

Does the rule cover the LLC as well?

Yes. The IRS says it applies to both credits from tax year 2026.

Verify with the IRS. For ITIN, joint filing or student visa questions, consult a CPA or attorney.
