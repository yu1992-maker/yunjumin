How Often Can You Earn a UK Bank Switching Bonus, and How Long Must an Old Account Be Closed?

Nov 11, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

There is no UK-wide rule; each bank sets its own. We break down once-only limits, look-back dates and group-wide exclusions, with real terms and a checklist.

the UK has no common rule on how long an account must be closed before you can earn another switching bonus. Every bank and every offer has its own terms. The three usual restrictions are: one bonus per person, no repeat bonus within a set period after a previous one, and treating anyone who has ever held an account as an existing customer. How many times you can earn a bonus depends entirely on the wording of the current offer.

Three common restrictions: know which one applies

TypeTypical wordingWhat it means
Once onlyBarclays' CASS offer terms say it can be used only onceOne bonus per person for that offer; closing the account does not reset it
Look-back (bonus received)Lloyds offer: no Lloyds, Halifax or Bank of Scotland switching bonus received since 1 January 2023The test is whether you received a bonus, not whether you held an account, and it uses a fixed date rather than "the past few years"
Look-back (account held)Some HSBC offers: no HSBC, First Direct or M&S Bank account held since 1 January 2018Holding an account at any point after the date disqualifies you

Most of these periods are fixed dates, such as 1 January 2023, not a rolling 12 months after closure. The answer to "how long since I closed it" therefore depends on the date in the terms, not on counting months yourself.

Examples from specific banks

  • Danske Bank: anyone who has benefited from this offer or another Danske personal current account switching offer since 1 January 2023 is ineligible.
  • Nationwide: it must be your first Nationwide switching bonus since 2021. The switch must also complete within 28 days, and a full switch includes closing the old account.
  • HSBC: one offer treated some former customers whose HSBC accounts were closed before 1 January 2023 as new customers; another excluded anyone who had held an account since 2018. Terms differ even within the same bank over time.
  • NatWest Group: roundups report that anyone who has received a NatWest, RBS or Ulster Bank switching bonus is excluded, a group-wide rule.
  • Barclays: anyone who has received a Barclays switching bonus is excluded, and its Premier offer has additional account-opening date limits.

One roundup says most banks exclude people who have had a switching bonus from the same bank in the past 2–3 years. That is a general claim for which we found no official basis. Sources disagree; the specific terms govern.

Why closing an account does not necessarily make you a new customer

  1. Terms often apply group-wide: Lloyds, Halifax and Bank of Scotland count as one; NatWest, RBS and Ulster count as one; HSBC, First Direct and M&S Bank are also listed together.
  2. Some terms test whether you received a bonus, others whether you held an account. The second is stricter.
  3. Forum users say First Direct keeps customer data for well over six years, so it can recognise former customers long after closure. This is a user claim, not official bank policy.
  4. Joint accounts do not double up: some offers state one payment per customer, not two through a joint account.

Checklist before trying for another bonus

  1. Find the current offer's Terms and Conditions (usually a PDF) and search for "previous", "since", "once per person" and "customer".
  2. Establish whether it is once-only or based on a cut-off date.
  3. Establish the scope of the exclusion: the brand only, or the whole group.
  4. Check whether it excludes people who received a bonus or people who held an account.
  5. Note your old account's opening date, closing date and any bonus received, and compare each with the dates in the terms.
  6. Confirm the switching conditions: whether you must use the Current Account Switch Service, how many Direct Debits are needed (Bank of Scotland requires at least 3 active DDs; roundups say Nationwide's offer needs at least 2 DDs or standing orders), and when the bonus is paid (Bank of Scotland says within 14 working days of the switch starting; Santander says within 90 days of requesting the switch).
  7. If in doubt, ask the bank and keep the written reply before you open the account.

Offers change quickly

Many £175 offers found in searches have expired. Barclays' £175 offer covered applications from 8 July to 30 August 2024; Danske's Switch & Spend ended on 30 June 2025; and Bank of Scotland's page showed its offer running until 29 June 2026. We found no £175 offer currently live with a confirmed end date, so check bank websites directly.

FAQ

My old account was closed a year ago. Can I earn another bonus?

There is no general answer. If the terms say "no bonus received since 1 January 2023", how long the account has been closed is irrelevant; what matters is whether you received a bonus after that date. If the offer is once-only, no length of time is enough.

Can I earn one bonus from each brand in the same group?

It depends on the terms. Lloyds, Halifax and Bank of Scotland offers combine all three brands, while different groups are usually treated separately.

What if the terms are unclear?

Ask the bank in writing and keep a screenshot of the reply. Do not rely on forum experience, because the same bank's terms can differ from one period to the next.
