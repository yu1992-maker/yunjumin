New Mexico LLC Certificate of Good Standing: How to Request One

Nov 7, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Request a New Mexico LLC Certificate of Good Standing online through the Secretary of State Enterprise portal: steps, fees (sources differ), expedite options and what to do if you cannot get one.

A New Mexico LLC's Certificate of Good Standing can be requested only through the Secretary of State's online portal, enterprise.sos.nm.gov. If the entity is Active, you pay and download it immediately. Most sources put the LLC fee at $25, but they differ, so rely on the amount shown at checkout. Banks, lenders and other states (for foreign qualification) commonly ask for it.

What it is and when you need it

Issued by the Secretary of State (SOS), it confirms the LLC legally exists and is in good standing in New Mexico. Typical uses:

  • Opening a business bank account (some banks)
  • Loans, financing and investor due diligence
  • Foreign qualification in another state
  • Reviews by payment platforms, buyers or licensing agencies

Distinguish it from the Taxation and Revenue Department's Letter of Good Standing, which certifies that New Mexico taxes are settled. The SOS also issues certificates of status and existence for other purposes (LegalZoom), so ask which one is required before ordering.

Before you apply: is the LLC in good standing?

  • LLCs file no annual or biennial report (per BusinessAnywhere and LegalZoom); corporations do.
  • A valid registered agent with a New Mexico street address must be maintained.
  • State fees must be paid; outstanding fees block the request (LegalZoom).
  • Only entities registered with the SOS qualify; sole proprietorships and general partnerships do not.

A lapsed registered agent is the most common cause of losing good standing. LegalZoom notes problems arise when an agent vacancy or change filing is about 30 days overdue.

Online steps

  1. Log in to enterprise.sos.nm.gov.
  2. Search the LLC by name or Business ID and open its page.
  3. Confirm the status is Active; otherwise the request option will not appear (LegalZoom).
  4. Click the Request Certificate icon at upper right.
  5. Choose Certificate of Good Standing.
  6. Enter contact details and number of copies.
  7. Pay; checkout shows the final fee.
  8. Download the PDF from the My Copies and Certificates Request work queue.

The SOS now requires business filings through the Enterprise portal and no longer accepts paper; third-party sites that mention mail-in forms may be out of date.

Validity: sources differ

  • BusinessAnywhere: the certificate stays downloadable in your work queue for 90 days; after that you must request again.
  • Another source says the certificate itself has no fixed expiry.
  • In practice, banks and lenders set their own recency rule, often 30 days, so request it once the requirement is clear rather than far in advance.

If you cannot obtain one

  1. Check whether the entity is marked other than Active.
  2. Confirm the registered agent is valid with a New Mexico address.
  3. File missing documents and pay outstanding fees.
  4. Complete reinstatement through the Business Services Division.
  5. Request the certificate again once restored.

Tips for banks and lenders

  • Ask which document they need: Certificate of Good Standing, Certificate of Existence, or just formation documents, the EIN letter and the operating agreement.
  • Download several PDFs so multiple institutions can use them within the validity window.
  • For foreign qualification, the other state may require a recently issued certificate; check its rules.

FAQ

Does a New Mexico LLC file an annual report?

No. BusinessAnywhere and LegalZoom say LLCs have no annual or biennial report; keep the registered agent current and fees paid. Corporations file biennial reports.

How much does it cost and how fast is it?

Most sources say $25 for an LLC; others cite $27 or a fee on top. Rely on checkout. Online payment usually means immediate download (LegalZoom); expedite options are $100 for two business days or $150 same day (BusinessAnywhere).

Does the bank want the SOS certificate or the tax letter?

Accounts and loans usually need the SOS Certificate of Good Standing. Proof that New Mexico taxes are settled is the Taxation and Revenue Department's Letter of Good Standing, requested separately.

Official sources govern (enterprise.sos.nm.gov and the Secretary of State website). We did not read the official fee page directly; rely on the portal for fees and process. For reinstatement, foreign qualification or loan terms, consult a CPA or attorney.
