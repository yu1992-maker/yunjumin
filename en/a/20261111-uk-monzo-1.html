Can You Open a Monzo Account Without a UK Address? Eligibility and ID Explained

Nov 11, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Monzo's official position: no UK address, no account. Eligibility, accepted ID, proof of address, the sign-up process, and alternatives if you are in North America.

without a UK address, Monzo will not open an account. Monzo's help page says "All you need is to live in the UK and have a UK address" and "if you don't have a UK address, we can't offer you an account". People living in the US or Canada with no UK residence cannot currently open a Monzo personal account.

Not being a UK tax resident, however, is not an obstacle. Monzo states that non-UK tax residents can open an account, provided they genuinely live in the UK and have a UK address.

Official eligibility

From Monzo's help centre page on opening a personal account:

  • Aged 16 or over;
  • Living in the UK full time, with the right to live there;
  • A valid UK address;
  • A supported phone operating system (the whole process happens in the app).

"Not a UK tax resident" versus "no UK address"

SituationMonzo's position
Living in the UK but not a UK tax residentEligible, provided you live in the UK and have a UK address
No UK addressNot eligible
Living in the US or Canada with no UK homeDoes not meet the "live in the UK" requirement

Replies on the Monzo community forum say the same: someone living in the US must first become a UK resident. Forum replies are user answers, not official terms. Another user says people who leave the UK long term should close their account under the terms; we could not verify that on an official page, so check Monzo's current terms.

Documents needed

1. Valid photo ID

  • Passport;
  • Driving licence (provisional accepted);
  • National identity card.

CitizenCard is not accepted because it only proves age. ID must be in date. An older Monzo blog also listed the BRP residence permit, but that article dates from 2018–19 and we did not see the same list on the current help page; confirm in the app.

2. Proof of address (only if Monzo asks)

  • Utility, landline or broadband bill from the last 3 months;
  • Official letter from DWP, Jobcentre, NHS or HMRC from the last 3 months (P45 and P60 excluded);
  • Current year's Council Tax bill;
  • Recent UK bank, building society, credit card, pension or investment statement;
  • Valid UK tenancy agreement;
  • Valid UK driving licence (if not already used as photo ID).

How to sign up

  1. Check your phone is supported; have your phone and valid ID ready.
  2. Download Monzo from the App Store or Google Play.
  3. Enter your address, email and phone number, and answer questions on employment and account purpose.
  4. Photograph your ID and record a short selfie video, which Monzo matches to the document.
  5. Choose an account type.
  6. The card usually arrives within days; activate it in the app.

Practical routes for people in North America

  • Not yet in the UK: sort out accommodation first. Once you have a UK address (tenancy, bills), apply.
  • Only need to receive pounds: Monzo cannot help. Other providers exist, but each restricts country of residence; read their terms first.
  • Business account: Monzo Business eligibility targets UK sole traders and limited-company directors, not people without UK business.

Where sources disagree

  • Some third-party guides claim digital banks "don't check residence". That conflicts with Monzo's official page; we follow the official page.
  • A 2019 Monzo blog described opening an account with no fixed address, but the current help page still requires a UK address; do not read it as permission to omit one.
  • We found no Doctor of Credit article on Monzo for non-residents, so none is cited.

FAQ

1. I hold a US green card and live in Florida. Can I open Monzo?

No. Monzo requires living in the UK with a UK address, regardless of immigration status.

2. Can I use a friend's UK address?

Not advisable. Monzo may ask you to prove you live there, and you must live in the UK. Misstating your address risks account closure.

3. I'm moving to the UK to study or work but am not a UK tax resident. Can I open an account?

Yes. Monzo says non-UK tax residents can apply if they live in the UK and have an address.

Monzo's official pages prevail and rules change. For complex tax, visa or cross-border money questions, consult a CPA or lawyer.
