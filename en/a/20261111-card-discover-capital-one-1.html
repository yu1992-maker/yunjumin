Discover Cards Moving to Capital One: Rewards, Limits and Billing

Nov 11, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Discover card accounts are moving in waves to Capital One’s website and app. What changes for redemptions, limits, billing, autopay and authorized users.

Discover credit cards are being moved in batches to Capital One's website and app. Most core terms stay the same: card number, $0 annual fee, APR, credit limit and account-opening date. What needs attention is redemption rules, autopay, new card numbers for authorized users and how you log in. Below is a checklist based on the sources we found.

Where the migration stands

  • Doctor of Credit reports that Discover told some cardholders their accounts would be managed on Capital One's website and app from July 27, 2026.
  • A November 4, 2026 update on the same site mentions another wave on January 18, 2027.
  • Capital One says accounts move in waves; an FAQ quoted by Money.com says through 2026 into early 2027.
  • There is no public list of which cards move in which wave. Go by the email or letter Discover or Capital One sends you.

Rewards: before and after

  1. If you want to redeem under the old rules, do it after the notice and before the switch. Applying rewards to the minimum payment, for example, will no longer be possible.
  2. If you spend rewards through Apple Pay, switch to another redemption method.
  3. After migration, Capital One Travel, Entertainment and Offers become available. Doctor of Credit says hotels and rental cars booked through Capital One Travel earn 5% cash back (5x miles on Discover it Miles); incidentals charged outside the platform earn the base rate.
  4. Whether rewards can move to other Capital One rewards accounts is unclear. Discover's email says only that you may be eligible, and only for some Capital One accounts; Doctor of Credit says there is no firm information and pooling is unlikely at the start. Do not plan transfers yet.

Will my limit change?

  • The migration itself does not change limits; that is supported by official statements.
  • Whether future credit-line increase policy changes is not addressed officially. Some blogs suggest Discover has been generous and Capital One conservative; treat that as speculation.
  • Suggestion: if you want an increase, request it through Discover's current channels first; afterwards, use whatever the Capital One app offers.

Billing and autopay

  1. Note your current due date. Money.com cites information that most due dates will not change; a few may move later, never earlier, to avoid technical problems, and affected customers will be notified.
  2. Check autopay before and after: funding account and amount option (minimum or full balance).
  3. If autopay does not carry over, Capital One will notify you by email or letter. Do not wait for the statement to find out.
  4. Update your address, phone and email in Discover, and confirm statement delivery (electronic or paper).
  5. If you set up automatic redemption, reports say you must reset the preference on Capital One's site.

Login and app

  • Download the Capital One app now. Some sources say you cannot sign in with a Discover account before migration, so there is no need to hunt for an entry point.
  • After the switch, add the Discover card to an existing Capital One login or create one. Discover will notify you when it is your turn.
  • Beware of phishing. Expect texts and emails impersonating Discover or Capital One; do not click links, open the official app or site yourself.

If you have authorized users

  1. New cards go to the primary cardholder, with advance notice.
  2. Authorized users can keep using old cards until the new ones arrive.
  3. Once they do, update subscriptions, Apple Pay and wallets tied to the old number.
  4. Authorized users can register their own Capital One online access.

FAQ

Will migration affect my credit score?

Capital One says no. The account is not reported as new and the open date stays, so account age is unaffected.

Will my Discover card become a Capital One card?

For now only website and app management moves; product name, annual fee and APR stay the same. There is no clear information on product-changing Discover cards into Capital One-branded cards. Separately, some Capital One cards (such as Quicksilver) are moving to the Discover network; that is a different change and does not involve your Discover card.

Will rewards expire?

Reportedly not while the account remains open, and balances carry over. Redemption rules change as described above; rely on the notice you receive.
