Wells Fargo Autograph: 3x categories, the welcome bonus and how it compares with Journey

Nov 9, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

No annual fee and 3x on dining, travel, gas and more. The official bonus is 20,000 points, though sources differ.

Wells Fargo Autograph is a Visa with no annual fee, earning 3x on restaurants, travel, gas and transit, streaming and phone plans, and 1x elsewhere. The official page shows a welcome bonus of 20,000 points after $1,000 in three months, which the bank values at about $200. Those who want a larger bonus and accept a fee may look at Autograph Journey.

The welcome bonus: sources differ

  • Official site: 20,000 points / $1,000, with no end date shown and a note that offers may differ by channel.
  • AwardWallet: also 20,000 / $1,000.
  • The Points Guy: has carried a 30,000 / $1,500 version; Bankrate's 30,000 offer expired in January 2023.

We can confirm only the 20,000 points currently shown on the official site. If someone cites more, check the application page first.

Using the 3x categories well

  1. Measure your spending. Add up restaurants, gas, transit, streaming, phone bills, flights and hotels as a share of the total.
  2. Put the phone bill on the card. It earns 3x and activates the phone protection.
  3. Use another card elsewhere. Everything else earns 1x; a flat-rate cash-back card does better.
  4. Point value. The bank prices one point at about one cent, so 3x is roughly a 3% return.

Redeeming points

  • Travel, statement credits or gift cards, at a base value of one cent per point.
  • The official site says statement credits post in 5–7 business days, bonus points become redeemable within 60 days of being earned, and points do not expire while the account is open.
  • According to The Points Guy, Autograph itself cannot transfer to airline or hotel partners; that belongs to Journey.

In short: for everyday spending without a fee, Autograph; for frequent hotel and flight bookings and an interest in transfers, consider Journey.

Before applying

  1. Confirm the current bonus and terms on the Wells Fargo site.
  2. Make sure $1,000 in three months fits your normal spending.
  3. If you hold another Wells Fargo card, note the official statement that a product change from another Wells Fargo card does not earn the welcome bonus or introductory APR.
  4. Pay on time and do not overspend to reach the bonus.

FAQ

Is there a cap on the 3x categories?

The official page lists none.

Is the bonus 20,000 or 30,000?

The official site currently shows 20,000 / $1,000. The 30,000 / $1,500 version can still be seen online, and Bankrate's expired in 2023; follow the application page.

Can points become airline miles?

According to The Points Guy, not with Autograph; Journey can.
