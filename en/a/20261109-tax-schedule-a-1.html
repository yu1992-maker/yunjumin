Florida Homebuyers: Property Tax and Mortgage Interest on Schedule A

Nov 9, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Property tax goes on line 5b and mortgage interest on line 8a. The SALT cap, Form 1098, points, escrow and how to split the closing statement.

Florida homebuyers deduct property tax and mortgage interest on Schedule A only if they itemize instead of taking the standard deduction. Property tax goes on line 5b, within the state and local tax (SALT) cap. Mortgage interest goes on 8a (reported on Form 1098), 8b (not reported) and 8c (points not on Form 1098). Figures are mainly from IRS pages for the 2025 filing season; recheck 2026 figures before filing.

First, is itemizing worth it?

Itemize only if property tax, mortgage interest and other deductions exceed the standard deduction. Florida has no state income tax, so SALT is mostly property tax, or sales tax if you elect it. Check the IRS figure for the year.

  • Add property tax, mortgage interest and charitable gifts, then compare.
  • Take the larger.
  • Only itemizers get a benefit from property tax and interest.

Which property taxes count

IRS Topic 503 allows state or local real property tax levied for the general public welfare at a like rate on all property in the jurisdiction.

The SALT cap

Topic 503 puts the combined cap at $40,000 ($20,000 married filing separately), reduced for higher MAGI but never below $10,000. It does not give the phaseout start or rate; see the Schedule A instructions. Third-party sources differ ($500,000 or $505,000), so we adopt neither. You may deduct state income tax or sales tax, not both.

The year you buy: splitting the closing statement

Under Publication 530, the seller is treated as paying tax up to the sale date and the buyer from it, whatever the local lien date.

  1. Find the property tax proration on the Closing Disclosure.
  2. Your deduction is full-year tax × days owned ÷ days in the tax year (Publication 530's example uses 122/365).
  3. If the seller paid your share and you did not reimburse it, reduce basis by that amount; you still deduct the tax.
  4. If you paid the seller's share without reimbursement, add it to basis; no deduction.
  5. Prior-year arrears you agree to pay also go to basis.

Watch escrow

Monthly escrow deposits are not tax paid. Deduct what your lender paid the county that year, per the tax bill.

Mortgage interest

Publication 936 (for 2025 returns):

  • For loans after December 15, 2017, the limit is interest on $750,000 of principal ($375,000 married filing separately).
  • Loans before December 16, 2017 use $1,000,000 ($500,000).
  • Lenders issue Form 1098 for interest of $600 or more, showing interest, mortgage insurance and, on a purchase, deductible points.
  • 1098 interest goes on 8a; other interest on 8b; unreported points on 8c.

Above $750,000, prorate: $750,000 ÷ average balance. The full 1098 amount is not all deductible.

Florida: homestead and your bill

  • Homestead exemption: the first $25,000 applies to all levies and the second $25,000 to non-school taxes (per real-estate and bank sites; check your county).
  • Save Our Homes caps annual assessment growth at the lower of 3% or CPI change, not applicable in year one.
  • Florida voted on a homestead amendment (Amendment 3) on November 3; we had no final result when writing. Check your county Property Appraiser.
  • You deduct tax actually paid, so homestead lowers it directly.

Checklist

  1. Closing Disclosure.
  2. Form 1098 (by late January).
  3. County tax bill, payment record and annual escrow statement.
  4. Compare itemizing with the standard deduction.
  5. Property tax on 5b; interest on 8a/8b/8c.

FAQ

With no state income tax, can I hit the SALT cap?

Yes, if property tax plus sales tax exceeds $40,000, mainly with expensive or multiple homes.

Can I deduct my monthly escrow?

No, only what the lender paid the county.

Where do I find points in the year of purchase?

Form 1098 first; if not there, the settlement statement, entered on 8c.
