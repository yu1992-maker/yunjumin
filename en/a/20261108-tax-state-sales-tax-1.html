Florida has no income tax: choosing the sales tax deduction

Nov 8, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

If you itemize, select state sales tax on Schedule A line 5 and estimate it with the IRS calculator or tables.

Florida has no state income tax, so on a federal return the state and local tax line of Schedule A can only be filled with state and local general sales tax. This matters only if you itemize; with the standard deduction the choice does not arise. The steps and methods follow. Use the latest IRS pages for figures.

First, is itemizing worthwhile?

The sales tax deduction is available only on Schedule A. Standard deductions, as reported by the IRS and several tax sites:

Filing status20252026 (published)
Single / married filing separately$15,750$16,100
Head of household$23,625$24,150
Married filing jointly$31,500$32,200

One source (Axios) gives $23,635 for head of household, probably a typo; most say $23,625. Additional amounts apply at 65 or with blindness.

A Florida filer usually needs mortgage interest, property tax, charitable gifts and sales tax that clearly exceed these figures. Homeowners with substantial interest and property tax are typical. Renters without large expenses are generally better off with the standard deduction.

Rules for the choice

  • State and local income tax and general sales tax are alternatives; you cannot deduct both.
  • Check the sales tax box on Schedule A, line 5a.
  • Real estate and personal property taxes are separate and unaffected.
  • With no state income tax, sales tax is in practice the only option in Florida.

Two methods

Method 1: IRS tables plus major purchases (most people)

The IRS Sales Tax Deduction Calculator estimates a year's sales tax from income, filing status, dependents and ZIP code. The IRS says:

  • Have ready your W-2s and 1099s, receipts for major purchases, ZIP codes and the dates you lived in each place.
  • It does not ask for your name, address, SSN or bank details, and does not store what you enter.
  • If a ZIP code covers several tax jurisdictions, it averages their rates.
  • Major purchases are not in the tables and are added separately. The IRS reasons that they do not recur every year and would produce a double benefit if included.

For which items qualify (vehicles, boats and so on), follow the IRS page and Schedule A instructions; we have not checked each category.

Method 2: Keep all receipts

The IRS page says you may always add up actual general sales tax from receipts. It can be higher in a year of heavy spending, but requires a full year of records. Without large purchases it is rarely worth the effort.

Filing checklist

  1. Total your itemized deductions: mortgage interest, property tax, gifts, excess medical costs and so on.
  2. Use the IRS calculator with your income and ZIP code.
  3. Add sales tax on a car, boat or major renovation bought that year.
  4. Enter the amount in the state and local tax section and check the 5a box.
  5. Compare with the standard deduction and take the larger. Software usually does this for you.

The SALT cap: sources differ

State and local taxes are capped in total, and here the sources conflict:

SourceStatement
IRS sales tax calculator pageCombined cap of $10,000 ($5,000 married filing separately)
NerdWallet, SoFi, SuperMoney and others on the new law (OBBBA)$40,000 for 2025 ($20,000 separately); about $40,400 for 2026; phased down above $500,000 of MAGI, but not below $10,000

Sources also differ on when the cap returns to $10,000 (after 2029, or from 2030). The calculator page may be out of date, so for a 2025 return read the cap in the 2025 Schedule A instructions and follow them. Most Florida households will not reach it, but check if property tax or income is high.

Points for Florida residents

  • With no state income tax there is no income tax to choose and no state refund to report.
  • If you moved to Florida during the year, the calculator asks for dates in each place and computes in segments.
  • If you do not itemize, ignore this and take the standard deduction.

Questions

In Florida, can I choose income tax instead?

Federally it is one or the other. With no state income tax, sales tax is in practice the only choice, provided you itemize.

Must I keep every receipt?

Not with the calculator or tables, apart from receipts for major purchases. Deducting actual amounts requires them all.

Can sales tax and property tax both be deducted?

Yes. Property tax is listed separately, but the total is subject to the SALT cap. See the section on differing sources for the figure.

For reference only; the IRS prevails. For large purchases, interstate moves or high income, consult a CPA or lawyer.
