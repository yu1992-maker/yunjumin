Amex MR to Aeroplan: ratio, timing and best uses

Nov 8, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Normally 1:1, officially up to 30 minutes and usually instant. Steps, U.S.-Canada transfers and cautions.

Amex Membership Rewards (MR) points normally transfer to Aeroplan at 1:1, with a minimum of 1,000 points. Prince of Travel lists the official time as "up to 30 minutes"; transfers are usually instant, with occasional delays in the past. Transfers cannot be reversed, so confirm award space on the Aeroplan site first.

Sources disagree on increments. Air Canada's page for U.S. Amex says a minimum of 1,000 in units of 1,000; some third parties say units of 100. Follow what your transfer page shows.

Transfer time: sources disagree

  • Prince of Travel: officially up to 30 minutes, in practice usually instant, with past delays.
  • Air Canada's Amex conversion page gives no processing time.

Do not transfer at the last moment before booking; allow at least an hour.

How to transfer

  1. Search your flight on aircanada.com with your Aeroplan account and confirm award space. Amex notes that it is not responsible for award availability.
  2. Check that the names on the Amex and Aeroplan accounts match and that the membership number is linked.
  3. Log in to the Membership Rewards page, choose Aeroplan and enter the points (from 1,000).
  4. Check your Aeroplan balance. If nothing arrives after 30 minutes, contact Amex.
  5. Ticket as soon as the points arrive, before the seat is taken.
Transfers are irreversible: points moved to Aeroplan cannot return to MR.

Transfer bonuses: current position

Amex sometimes runs transfer bonuses, but not on a schedule. As of the date of this article (November 8, 2026), the Awardtravelfinder tracker we found (updated July 20, 2026) showed no active Aeroplan bonus; the last Amex-to-Aeroplan bonus it recorded (U.S., +20%) expired in September 2021. An aggregator also listed a Flying Blue +25% offer ending October 31, 2026, now expired. Such data can lag, so check the Amex offers page before transferring.

There is no need to hold off waiting for a bonus. Aeroplan award space changes quickly; if you have a trip that needs it, transfer.

When it is good value

  • Partner awards: Aeroplan has removed fuel surcharges on partner airlines, though each passenger pays a C$39 booking fee per partner award (Prince of Travel and related reports). In a Lufthansa first class example from Chicago to Frankfurt, the old price was 70,000 miles plus about US$820 in surcharges; the new price is 100,000 miles and no surcharge.
  • Price changes: one source says Aeroplan adjusted partner award prices on June 1, 2026, with business class from Australia going up. Rely on the live price.
  • Avoid cheap tickets: on inexpensive fares, an MR point used against cash is often worth more than one converted for short-haul economy.
  • Premium cabins: business, first and long-haul international usually give the best value.

Can U.S. Amex points move to a Canadian Aeroplan account?

Air Canada lists U.S. Amex MR to Aeroplan at 1:1, online or by calling 1-800-297-3276. According to a March 2024 Prince of Travel article, you can also move U.S. MR points to a Canadian MR account (1:1) by international transfer and then to Aeroplan:

  • one international transfer every 12 months;
  • call U.S. Amex with both card numbers;
  • minimum 1,000 points;
  • about 1 to 2 weeks; online chat may not be able to do it.

That article is older and Amex may have changed the rules. Confirm with Amex first.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a transfer take?

Officially up to 30 minutes; usually instant, with occasional delays.

Can I reverse it?

No. Points sent to Aeroplan cannot return to MR, so check seats first.

What is the minimum?

1,000 points. Sources disagree on whether increments are 100 or 1,000; follow the transfer page.

Reminder: ratios, minimums, bonuses and Aeroplan prices change at any time. Rely on Amex and Air Canada, and consult a CPA or lawyer for cross-border tax or complex matters.
