UK Experian statutory credit report: free, with ID and address history

Nov 8, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

How to request it, what documents you need, how to handle six years of addresses, and common obstacles for newcomers.

Experian's statutory credit report in the UK is free and can be requested online or by post. You must consent to identity checks and should list every UK address and postcode from the past six years. It does not include a credit score; for a score and the full report, use Experian's other products or a free app.

Sources differ on cost: some third-party pages mention an old £2 charge, while Experian's own page now says free. We follow Experian and the ICO, but confirm at experian.co.uk before applying.

What to prepare

  1. Name, date of birth, current address, matching your UK bank and utility records.
  2. Six years of addresses. Experian recommends listing them all with postcodes so that the report matches what lenders see.
  3. Consent to identity checks. Online applications must accept identity verification and anti-fraud screening.
  4. Documents for postal applications. Third-party guides generally mention a copy of photo ID and a recent utility bill or bank statement. Follow Experian's form; we could not read its full text.

Applying online

  1. Type experian.co.uk directly and find the Statutory Credit Report page. Avoid advertisements for "free credit report", which can lead to paid lookalike sites.
  2. Enter personal details and address history.
  3. Accept the identity verification terms and submit.
  4. Wait for the passkey by post. Experian says it is sent within five working days.
  5. Use the passkey to log in to the secure page and read the report.

Applying by post

  1. Download and complete Experian's Statutory Application Form.
  2. Enclose the required evidence.
  3. Send it to Experian Ltd, Customer Support Centre, PO Box 8000, Nottingham, NG80 7WF.
  4. Experian says postal reports usually arrive within seven working days, longer if it needs more information to verify you.

If you lack six years of addresses

  • New arrivals should list the addresses they have. Do not invent any.
  • On the MoneySavingExpert forum some users report that the obstacle is usually identity verification rather than the number of years. That is user experience, not official guidance.
  • If online verification fails, try the postal route with ID and proof of address, or contact Experian.
  • If your address is outside the UK, sites that require a UK postcode may not accept the form; contact Experian directly.

Other free routes

  • Experian's own free app or account shows the Experian score and report, according to Which? and MSE coverage.
  • MSE Credit Club has used TransUnion data since November 2023 and no longer provides an Experian report.
  • ClearScore shows Equifax data free of charge.

Experian, Equifax and TransUnion may hold different information, so check all three.

What to look for

  1. Errors in personal details, or addresses you do not recognize.
  2. Accounts you do not recognize.
  3. Late payments recorded in error.
  4. Searches by unfamiliar organizations, a possible sign of identity misuse.

Questions

Is the statutory report really free?

The ICO says no payment is required, and Experian's page says it is free to apply. If a site asks for bank card details, it may be a subscription product or an imitation.

How long does it take?

Online, Experian says the passkey is sent within five working days. By post, the report usually arrives within seven. Verification problems add time.

Does it show my score?

No. Use a free Experian account or app, or a paid product. Scores from different platforms use different scales and cannot be compared directly.

Reminder: Experian and the ICO prevail, and rules and prices may change. For identity misuse, credit disputes or cross-border tax and immigration matters, consult a CPA or lawyer.
