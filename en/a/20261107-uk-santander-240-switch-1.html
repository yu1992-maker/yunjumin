Santander's £240 Switch Offer: Can Non-UK Residents Get It?

Nov 7, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

The offer requires UK residence. Conditions, exclusions, alternatives for those in the US or Canada, and tax reminders.

Generally no. Santander's press release says the £240 switcher offer is for UK residents moving an account from another provider through the Current Account Switch Service (CASS). Someone living in the US or Canada without a UK address will normally not qualify, and nothing we found mentions an exception for non-residents.

What the offer is

It applies to Santander UK's Everyday Current Account, not the Edge range. According to the official press release it covers switches started on or after September 1, 2026 and can be withdrawn at any time.

  • Reward: £240 in cash.
  • Linked product: Santander Regular Saver at 8% AER/gross (variable), including a 5% bonus rate for 12 months.
  • Method: the UK Current Account Switch Service.

Who is excluded

  • Anyone where a named account holder held a Santander current account on January 1, 2026.
  • Anyone who has received a Santander switching reward before.
  • Switches from Santander, cahoot or Cater Allen.
  • Non-UK residents.

Joint accounts may take part. Depositscout says the £240 is paid per account, not per person.

Why non-residents have little chance

  1. The offer terms require UK residence. That is stated in the press release.
  2. Santander's general policy points the same way. Search excerpts of its Brexit information page say new Santander UK products require UK residence. We could not open the page itself, so please verify.
  3. CASS is a domestic mechanism. You need an existing UK current account to switch from. We found no explicit statement on non-residents using it; this point is an inference.
  4. The required Direct Debits are UK household bills. Two are hard to arrange without a UK address.

Where sources differ

  • Dates. One Money to the Masses article says applications were due by October 7, 2026. Depositscout and Money Facts Compare say the offer ended that day and reopened, with applications from October 27 eligible and no closing date. Santander says it may be withdrawn at any time. Check the official terms.
  • Payment time. Santander says 30 days after assessment; Money Facts Compare says 90 days after application. The two are broadly consistent.
  • Residence. Money Facts Compare and Depositscout do not mention it, but the press release does. The official text governs.
  • Full PDF terms. We could not read these. The details above come from the press release and media coverage.

If you have already moved to the US or Canada

  1. Establish whether you are still a UK resident. Long-term residence abroad normally means you are not.
  2. If you still have a UK home and account, ask Santander about your position first and keep the written reply.
  3. Do not give a false UK address or residence. It may breach bank terms and lead to closure of the account.
  4. If you need a UK account anyway, compare accounts designed for overseas residents, which usually carry minimum deposit or income requirements.
  5. Santander's EU Basic Account is for EU residents, not those in the US or Canada. The form we saw dates from 2019; check current rules.

Tax if you do receive it

These are general reminders. We found no tax guidance specific to this reward.

  • US tax residents, including green card holders, generally report worldwide income. Bank bonuses and savings interest are commonly treated as interest.
  • If foreign accounts exceed US$10,000 in total at any time in the year, FinCEN 114 (FBAR) is required.
  • Form 8938 (FATCA) may apply, with thresholds that depend on status.
  • Canadian residents have foreign asset reporting rules too; ask a Canadian accountant.

FAQ

I live in the US but have a UK phone number and an old address. Can I apply?

Not advisable. The test is residence, not a phone number. An application could be refused or the account closed later.

Does a green card make me resident?

A green card is a US status and has no bearing on UK residence, which is what Santander requires.

Does the Edge account qualify for £240?

The £240 offer we found is for the Everyday Current Account. For Edge Up we saw only older £150 or £175 rewards.

Reminder

Bank terms change often. This article reflects November 7, 2026. Verify with Santander, and consult a CPA or attorney on tax reporting or residence.
