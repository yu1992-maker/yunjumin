Proof of address for UK banks: how recent and which documents work

Nov 8, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Most banks want documents under 3 months old. What works, what does not, and options for new arrivals with no bills.

UK banks ask for proof of address because anti-money-laundering rules require them to verify who you are, and address is part of that. The usual package is ID such as a passport plus a utility bill or bank statement no more than 3 months old showing your UK address. Each bank sets its own time limits and document list. What follows is drawn from several banks' sites and third-party summaries.

Why banks insist

The Money Laundering Regulations 2017 require financial firms to carry out customer due diligence. HMRC's supervision manual refers to the document hierarchy in the JMLSG industry guidance (from paragraph 5.3.36) and says bank statements in the customer's name can verify an address. How recent a document must be is left to each firm's policy, which is why banks differ.

What works and what does not

  1. Usually accepted: electricity, gas, water, landline or broadband bills, bank or card statements, Council Tax bills, tenancy agreements and mortgage statements.
  2. Usually refused: mobile phone bills. Barclays basic accounts and Cynergy Bank both exclude them.
  3. Cynergy Bank also refuses PO box addresses and self-printed web statements.
  4. One document cannot do both jobs. Cynergy says the same document cannot prove identity and address; a third-party summary attributes a similar rule to Starling.
  5. Name and address must match the application exactly. A missing flat number can be enough for rejection.

New arrivals with no bills

Many newcomers are stuck in a loop: no bill, no account; no account, no bill. Common ways out:

  • Tenancy agreement. Acceptable types depend on the bank. Barclays lists council or housing association tenancies; ask about private landlords.
  • Letter from family. Wise's article says some banks accept a letter from a relative you live with who is already a customer. Students can use proof of enrollment.
  • An online account first. Wise and Revolut are more flexible. Wise's U.S. page says verification with U.S. identity and address documents is possible before arrival, though more may be requested.
  • Something that generates a bill. Set up broadband or utilities and apply after the first statement.

Opening from the U.S.

The large banks (Barclays, Lloyds, HSBC, NatWest) generally require a UK address. Wise's article says their international accounts target wealthy clients, with large deposits, minimum monthly funding and sometimes fees. A commercial site gives HSBC Expat's threshold as £50,000 of assets or £100,000 of income; we did not find the official text.

Sources conflict on whether Monzo and Starling can be opened without a UK address. Some say it is required; some older pages list them as needing no proof of residence. Check the banks' current requirements. U.S. tax residents will usually also complete a FATCA self-certification, according to third-party sources that we could not match to a bank page.

Submitting documents

  1. Find the bank's "acceptable documents" page for your account type.
  2. Prepare a passport or driving license for identity.
  3. Choose a recent document with your full name and UK address, dated within 3 months.
  4. Photograph the whole page, with all four corners, the date and the address visible.
  5. If certified paper copies are required, confirm who may certify. Cynergy, for non-UK residents, wants certification by a UK bank, solicitor, accountant or post office, also dated within 3 months.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a mobile phone bill?

Usually not. Barclays basic accounts and Cynergy Bank exclude them. A broadband or electricity bill or a bank statement is safer.

My bill is more than 3 months old. What now?

It will generally be refused. Use a document with a longer limit, such as a Council Tax bill or tenancy agreement (12 months on the Barclays list), or wait for the next bill.

Are downloaded PDF statements accepted?

It varies. Cynergy refuses self-printed web statements, and one employer page asks for paper originals, though that is that institution's rule. If the bank is unclear, ask first or use an officially issued e-statement.
