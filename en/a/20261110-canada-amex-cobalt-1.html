Amex Cobalt Welcome Bonus: How Much to Spend Each Month to Earn All 15,000 Points

Nov 10, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

The Amex Cobalt (Canada) bonus pays up to 15,000 points: 1,250 for each statement month with $750 in net spend during the first year. Rules, tactics and eligibility.

The Amex Cobalt (Canada) welcome offer is currently up to 15,000 Membership Rewards points. New cardmembers earn 1,250 points for each billing month in the first 12 months with at least $750 in net purchases, so the full bonus requires $9,000 of spending (12 × $750). It accrues month by month: miss the threshold in a given month and that month's 1,250 points are lost.

Note: compiled from public sources including Prince of Travel (page updated 13 February 2026) and Milesopedia (updated 29 October 2026). Amex can change terms at any time; check the current offer on Amex Canada's website before applying.

Milesopedia notes that spending in any category counts towards the threshold, including 1x purchases. It estimates first-year value at about $255 (the site's own estimate, not an official figure).

How each month is counted

  1. Check your statement date: the bonus runs on billing periods, not calendar months.
  2. Reach $750 in net purchases (after refunds) within each billing period.
  3. After that period closes, 1,250 points are awarded.
  4. Repeat for 12 periods, up to 15,000 points.

The exact dates of each billing period follow your card's statement date; we found no more detailed definition, so check your Amex account.

How to earn the full bonus reliably

  • Concentrate everyday spending on the card. Groceries, restaurants and food delivery are already Cobalt's high-earning categories, and $750 is achievable for most households.
  • Track progress before the statement date. Check the app; if you are short, move planned bills (phone, insurance, subscriptions) into the current period.
  • Do not count on refunds. The threshold is net spend, so returns reduce it.
  • Pay in full every month. Interest quickly erodes the value of the points.

The cost: the fee

According to Milesopedia, the monthly fee rose from $12.99 to $15.99 on 5 November 2025 (Quebec: annual fee from $150 to $191.88). For new applicants, at the $15.99 monthly fee Amex published in September 2026, the annual cost is about $191.88. Prince of Travel lists a $192 annual fee charged at $15.99 per month. Weigh this against the 15,000 points before applying.

FAQ

1. Is the bonus paid at once or monthly?

Monthly: 1,250 points for each billing period with $750 in net spend, up to 12 times.

2. Can I make up a missed month later?

Available sources describe the bonus as calculated per period and say nothing about catching up. We found no make-up provision; confirm with Amex.

3. I had a Cobalt before. Am I eligible?

No. The terms quoted by Milesopedia exclude current and former cardmembers. Amex's site lets you check eligibility before applying without affecting your credit score.

We found no information on whether the Quebec offer differs; check the official site.
