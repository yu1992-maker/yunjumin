Wells Fargo checking bonuses: direct deposit and the Clear Access requirements

Nov 9, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

The current $125 Clear Access bonus, its direct deposit and fee-waiver rules, and the terms of the earlier Everyday Checking offer.

The current official offer we could find is the $125 Clear Access Banking bonus: open with at least $25 and complete at least 10 qualifying transactions within 60 days. No direct deposit is required. The bonus that did require it was the $400/$500 Everyday Checking offer ($1,000 in qualifying electronic deposits within 90 days); the deadline we found was October 6, 2026, so it has expired. Check the official site for a newer version.

1. Clear Access $125: official terms

From Wells Fargo's offer page (accountoffers.wellsfargo.com/checking125):

ItemTerms
Bonus$125
Opening depositAt least $25
TransactionsAt least 10 posted qualifying transactions within 60 calendar days of opening
Open byJanuary 13, 2027, using the offer code
PaymentWithin 30 calendar days of qualifying, if the account is not closed or restricted
TaxThe bonus is income and you are responsible for reporting it

What counts

  • Counts: posted Zelle transfers, mobile check deposits, Bill Pay payments, and eligible debit card purchases, including digital wallets and recurring charges.
  • Does not count: ATM and teller transactions, pending transactions, and transactions made with the account and routing number.

2. Does Clear Access need direct deposit?

Not for the bonus. Direct deposit matters only for waiving the monthly fee, which is $5. The official page lists three ways; any one suffices:

  1. The primary owner is 13–24 years old (ages 13–16 need an adult co-owner).
  2. At least $250 in qualifying electronic deposits each fee period, such as ACH direct deposit or certain instant payments. Transfers, Zelle, mobile deposits and branch or ATM deposits do not count.
  3. A qualifying monthly military direct deposit with enrollment in Worldwide Military Banking.

Some third-party descriptions of the waiver differ from the official page; follow the official page.

3. The bonus that needed direct deposit: Everyday Checking $500

Doctor of Credit lists this offer as ended, with an opening deadline of October 6, 2026:

  • Open a new Everyday Checking account with the code for $500.
  • Receive at least $1,000 in qualifying electronic deposits within 90 days: ACH direct deposit, RTP or FedNow instant payments, or certain card network credits. Transfers, Zelle, mobile deposits and branch or ATM deposits do not count.
  • Early Pay Day funds count only once posted, not while pending.
  • The bonus is paid within 30 days of qualifying and the account must be open at that time.
  • The monthly fee is $15, with several waivers, for example $500 in qualifying electronic deposits, a $1,500 minimum daily balance, or age 17–24.

Sources differ: others mention earlier versions requiring $3,000 in direct deposits or paying $325/$400, and one page says the offer was extended to January 19, 2027, which conflicts with October 6. We could not verify this on an official page.

4. Who is eligible

  • The primary account owner only, as a new personal checking customer.
  • Not those who received a Wells Fargo personal checking bonus in the past 12 months.
  • Not Wells Fargo employees, nonresident aliens or foreign entities with a W-8 on file, or holders of Private Bank deposit accounts.
  • Online applicants must be 18 or older; ages 13–17 must open at a branch.
  • Opening requires a Social Security number, valid ID (driver's license, state ID or consular ID) and a current address.
  • One bonus per customer or account, not combinable with other personal deposit offers.

5. Steps

  1. Open the official offer page, confirm the offer is live and note the code.
  2. Enter the code when opening and deposit at least $25.
  3. Activate the debit card and mobile app.
  4. Spread at least 10 qualifying transactions over 60 days: small debit purchases, Bill Pay, Zelle, mobile check deposits. Exclude ATM and account-and-routing-number transactions.
  5. To waive the fee, set up at least $250 a month in ACH direct deposit or confirm another waiver.
  6. Wait up to 30 days after qualifying and keep the account open.
  7. Keep screenshots of the confirmation and transactions in case you need to query a missing bonus.

6. FAQ

Does the $125 require direct deposit?

No. The terms ask only for a $25 opening deposit and 10 qualifying transactions. Direct deposit is one way to waive the $5 fee.

Do Zelle and mobile deposits count as direct deposit?

No. Zelle, transfers, mobile deposits and branch or ATM deposits are not qualifying electronic deposits. Zelle and mobile deposits can count toward the 10 transactions.

Is the bonus taxable?

Yes. The official page says it is income and that reporting is your responsibility; banks generally report it to the IRS.

Information as of November 9, 2026. Offers may change at any time. Wells Fargo's official terms prevail; consult a CPA or attorney on tax or complex matters.
