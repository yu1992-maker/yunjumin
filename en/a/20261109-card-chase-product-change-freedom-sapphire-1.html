Chase product changes: can Freedom become Sapphire?

Nov 9, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

A change is possible but earns no bonus. Under the 2026 rules, each Sapphire bonus is read as once per lifetime.

You can ask Chase to product-change a Freedom card to Sapphire Preferred, but the change earns no welcome bonus and approval is at Chase's discretion. To earn a bonus you generally need a new Sapphire application while keeping the Freedom.

Chase has changed these rules several times and many articles are outdated. We use only what sources support, and mark disagreements.

What a product change is

  • It moves the same account to another Chase card. History and opening date are generally kept and there is no new hard inquiry. This is common industry understanding; Chase does not spell it out, so confirm with the bank.
  • A personal card cannot become a business card, or the reverse (Million Mile Secrets).
  • It is not automatic and depends on the account (same source).

The 2026 Sapphire bonus rules

These decide whether to change or apply. Upgraded Points gives this timeline:

  1. August 2017: the One Sapphire rule, one Sapphire at a time.
  2. August 2018: the bonus wait lengthens from 24 to 48 months.
  3. June 23, 2025: One Sapphire and the 48-month rule end; both cards may be held, with a pop-up check on eligibility.
  4. January 22, 2026: one bonus per lifetime for each Sapphire card; holding the other does not matter.

Chase's Sapphire Preferred terms say you cannot apply if you hold the card now, that the bonus "may" be unavailable if you held it before, and that it may consider how many cards you have opened and closed. That is looser than the press; "once per lifetime" is the media reading, and the pop-up result governs.

Change or apply

When a change fits

  • You have already earned the Preferred bonus and want only the benefits.
  • Your Freedom is well aged and you prefer to avoid a hard inquiry.
  • The bonus does not matter to you.

When a new application fits

  • You have never earned it. Chase shows 75,000 points after $5,000 in the first three months.
  • You want to keep Freedom's cash-back categories. Since several Sapphire cards may be held, there is no need to downgrade an old card to make room.

A sensible order:

  1. Check the pop-up on Chase's application page (Points Guy says this does not affect your score).
  2. Check 5/24. Upgraded Points says Chase still limits applicants to no more than five new personal cards in 24 months, and about two in 30 days.
  3. Apply if eligible; if not, consider a product change.

Fees and bonus: sources differ

  • Chase currently shows a $95 fee and 75,000 points.
  • NerdWallet reported a limited 100,000-point offer from June 15, 2026, and another source mentioned changes to the fee and hotel credit; both differ from the official page.

These figures move. Rely on Chase's site on the day.

How to request a change

  1. Send a secure message or call the number on the card and name the target card.
  2. Ask about the fee, when it is charged, refunds and existing points.
  3. Confirm whether any bonus applies (usually none).
  4. Check the next statement for the fee and benefits.

Questions

Can I still earn a Sapphire bonus after a change?

The change carries none. Whether a later application does depends on Chase's check; the old 24- and 48-month rules are out of date.

Does Freedom disappear?

Yes. One account is one card. To hold both, apply for Sapphire.

Should I change before applying?

No. "Change, then wait a few days" comes from user reports and old articles, not Chase, and we do not recommend it.
