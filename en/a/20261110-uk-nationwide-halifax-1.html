Nationwide vs Halifax Switching Bonuses: Which Should a Newcomer Choose?

Nov 10, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Nationwide's site still lists a £175 switch bonus; we could not confirm a live Halifax offer, and Halifax is being folded into Lloyds.

on the evidence we found, newcomers should look at Nationwide first. Its switching page still shows a £175 reward (limited time, terms apply). We could not confirm any current Halifax offer, and the Halifax brand is being merged into Lloyds. Both types of reward generally require UK residence, a UK address and a switch through the Current Account Switch Service (CASS), so readers in the US or Canada should check eligibility first.

Nationwide's £175: conditions on the site

  1. Switch from a non-Nationwide, non-Virgin Money current account and choose a full switch.
  2. Complete the switch within 28 days, move at least two Direct Debits and ask Nationwide to close the old account.
  3. Within 31 days of opening (or of requesting a switch into an existing account), pay in £1,000 and make a debit-card payment; "restrictions apply".
  4. One Nationwide switching reward per account type (sole or joint).
  5. Payment within 10 days once all conditions are met.

The page does not state a residence requirement, what the restrictions are, or an end date. The full terms are in the linked PDF; read them before applying.

Conflicting information

  • Is the Nationwide offer still live? A Nationwide press release said the £175 offer would close at 23:59 on 4 March 2026, yet the switching page we checked still shows £175, and Be Clever With Your Cash lists it as ongoing. It may have been relaunched, but we found no announcement, so this is uncertain.
  • A £370 figure in search results: reports mention a larger October 2026 offer requiring three Direct Debits and £2,000. We could not verify which bank or whether it is real.
  • Halifax amounts: Which? reported a Halifax £225 offer (£150 switch + £75 loyalty) ending in April 2026; Be Clever With Your Cash currently lists no Halifax switch offer. Most likely none is live; check the bank.
  • Halifax Reward account: some articles say the £5 monthly reward and cinema tickets were withdrawn, others that the account was cut, while Halifax's site still advertises up to £60 a year after switching.

Cross-exclusions

  • Search results indicate Nationwide's reward excludes switches from Halifax or Bank of Scotland.
  • Lloyds Banking Group offers typically exclude switches from Halifax or Bank of Scotland and anyone who received a Lloyds, Halifax or Bank of Scotland switching reward since 1 January 2023 (from search snippets; check the terms).

Step by step

  1. Confirm you have a UK address, UK ID and an existing UK current account to switch via CASS. Without one, most switching rewards are out of reach.
  2. Open Nationwide's switching page, use "Check if you're eligible" and read the full PDF terms.
  3. Have at least two active Direct Debits and £1,000 ready to pay in within 31 days.
  4. Make a debit-card payment soon after opening; do not leave it to the last day.
  5. For Halifax, check its switching and Rates and Charges pages or call 0345 602 0000 (Mon–Fri 9:00–17:30 UK time) to confirm any offer and the Lloyds migration.

FAQ

Can I qualify from the US with no UK address?

The page states no residence rule, but switching runs through CASS and requires moving an existing UK current account, so in practice you need a UK address and account. Confirm with the bank.

Will Halifax accounts disappear?

According to Be Clever With Your Cash, the brand moves to Lloyds while account numbers, card numbers and sort codes stay the same; you will later use the Lloyds app.

Is the reward taxable?

We found no official UK guidance specific to these rewards. If you are also a US tax resident, bank bonuses are generally reportable in the US; consult a CPA.

Offers change without notice; rely on Nationwide's and Halifax/Lloyds' official sites. For cross-border tax or residency questions, consult a CPA or attorney.
