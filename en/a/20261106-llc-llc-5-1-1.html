Florida LLC annual report: the 1 May deadline, fees and late consequences

Nov 6, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Florida LLCs file an annual report between 1 January and 1 May for $138.75. Late filers pay a $400 fee, and non-filers are dissolved in September. Steps, checklist and FAQ.

Every Florida LLC must file an annual report on Sunbiz between 1 January and 1 May, for $138.75. Filing after 1 May adds a $400 late fee, for $538.75 in total. LLCs that still have not filed by September are administratively dissolved. The next deadline is 1 May 2027.

Note: the Florida Department of State site (dos.fl.gov) could not be reached for this update. The amounts and process below come from several third-party guides that agree with each other; check the figures on Sunbiz before filing.

1. Key dates and fees

ItemDetail
Filing window1 January to 1 May each year, starting the year after formation
Standard fee$138.75
Late fee$400 (reportedly not waivable), $538.75 in total
Optional certificate of status$5
Correcting a filed report$50 (amended annual report)
Administrative dissolutionClose of business on the fourth Friday of September
Reinstatement$100 plus $138.75 for each unfiled year

Filing after 11:59 pm Eastern on 1 May counts as late. A Florida LLC has no separate state franchise-style fee; this is the annual cost.

2. Filing online

  1. Find the company's Document Number on Sunbiz.
  2. Open the Sunbiz annual report page and enter it.
  3. Review and update the principal office, mailing address, registered agent, managers or members, and EIN.
  4. Have a manager or managing member sign electronically.
  5. Pay $138.75 (plus $5 for a certificate of status if needed).
  6. Save the confirmation page and email.

3. What to have ready

  • Document Number
  • Principal office address; the mailing address may be a P.O. Box
  • EIN (FEI number); if not applied for or not applicable, choose the option the page offers
  • Registered agent name and Florida street address (an LLC cannot be its own registered agent)
  • Name, address and title of at least one manager or managing member
  • A credit or debit card; Sunbiz also accepts cheques, money orders and E-File accounts

4. What the report can and cannot change

  • Can change: member or manager names and addresses, registered agent, principal office, EIN.
  • Cannot change: the company name, which requires articles of amendment.

5. Payment timing

According to BusinessAnywhere, card payments take about 2–3 days to process and cheques about 3–5 weeks; mailed cheques must be postmarked by 1 May. Near the deadline, pay online by card so processing delays do not make you late.

6. If you miss it

  1. From 2 May: the $400 late fee applies automatically.
  2. Before the third Friday of September: you can still file, for $538.75.
  3. Fourth Friday of September: unfiled LLCs are administratively dissolved.
  4. After dissolution: file for reinstatement, $100 plus $138.75 for each unfiled year.

Per BusinessAnywhere, LLCs dissolved for less than a calendar year can reinstate online by card with immediate updates; beyond a year it takes 2–3 business days while the state checks the name is still available. While dissolved, the company's standing suffers, which can complicate bank accounts, contracts and loans.

7. Is a federal BOI report still required?

US domestic companies no longer file beneficial ownership information with FinCEN. FinCEN's final rule of August 2026 made the exemption for US companies and US persons permanent; sources differ on the date, citing 11 or 14 August. Foreign-registered companies must still file a BOIR within 30 days of their US registration becoming effective and update within 30 days of changes. The Florida annual report is a separate obligation and is still required.

FAQ

My LLC was formed this year. Do I file this year?

According to BusinessAnywhere, reports start in the year after formation. An LLC formed in November 2026 files its first report between 1 January and 1 May 2027.

No business and no income. Can I skip it?

No. As long as the company is active with the state, the report is due. To stop operating, dissolve formally rather than let it lapse into late fees and administrative dissolution.

If 1 May falls on a weekend or holiday, is there an extension?

None of the sources we found mention one. Do not gamble: file by mid-April and keep the confirmation.

Reminder

Official sources govern, and fees and dates can change each year. For reinstatement after dissolution, layered ownership, foreign owners or tax questions, consult a Florida CPA or lawyer.
