Wells Fargo Credit Card Application Rules: 4 Months, 48 Months, and ITIN

Nov 21, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Wells Fargo's current terms: opening a WF card in the last 4 months may block approval; holding or opening the same card within 48 months may cost you the bonus. ITIN eligibility is disputed.

before applying for a Wells Fargo card, remember two rules from the official terms: ① if you opened a Wells Fargo consumer credit card in the last 4 months, you may not be approved for another; ② if you have, or opened, the same card product in the last 48 months, you may not get the welcome bonus or intro APR—even if that account is closed with a $0 balance.

Compiled from the application terms on Wells Fargo's card pages (checked November 21, 2026) and public sources; the official site prevails.

Rule 1: one card every 4 months

In Wells Fargo's words, you may not qualify for an additional Wells Fargo-branded consumer credit card if you opened one in the last 4 months. Many older guides still say “6 months” (Doctor of Credit reported that rule in 2022); the current terms say 4. Some readers report uneven enforcement, but to be safe, space Wells Fargo cards at least 4 months apart.

Rule 2: 48 months per product for bonuses

If you currently hold the card you're applying for, or opened it within the last 48 months, you may not be eligible for the welcome bonus, intro APR or fee offers—even if the account is closed. In practice, each card's bonus is available about once every four years. Different products (say, Active Cash first, Autograph later) aren't affected by each other under this rule.

Rule 3: product changes don't earn bonuses

Wells Fargo's help pages say switching from one Wells Fargo card to another does not come with a new welcome bonus or new intro APR. To get a bonus you need a new application.

Can you apply with an ITIN?

Sources disagree:

  • WalletHub and some newer guides say Wells Fargo accepts an ITIN in place of an SSN for credit cards;
  • Doctor of Credit's ITIN guide says Wells Fargo does not (that page's update date is unclear and may be outdated).

We found no explicit statement on Wells Fargo's own site. Ask at a branch or call first, and only use an ITIN where the application clearly allows it—never type an ITIN into a field that only accepts an SSN. If you only have an ITIN, starting with banks that clearly accept ITINs is the safer route.

Improve your approval odds

  • Open a Wells Fargo checking account and build a relationship first (a common practice, not an official requirement);
  • Leave at least 4 months between Wells Fargo cards;
  • Pay existing cards on time and in full, and keep utilization low.

FAQ

Do business cards count toward the 4 months?

The official wording covers “Wells Fargo-branded consumer credit cards.” Doctor of Credit said in 2022 that personal and business cards were counted separately; check the terms when you apply.

How long after a denial can I reapply?

Wells Fargo doesn't publish a fixed period. Call reconsideration to ask why, then reapply after fixing the issue or improving your credit.
