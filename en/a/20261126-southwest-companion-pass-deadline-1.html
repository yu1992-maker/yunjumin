Southwest Companion Pass: the December 2 approval deadline and 135,000 points

Nov 26, 20265 min readYunjumin Forum editors

From 2027, card bonuses stop counting toward the Companion Pass. The December 2 cutoff, the statement-date trap and the personal-plus-business pairing.

2026 is the last year of Southwest's current Companion Pass rules. Earn 135,000 Companion Pass qualifying points (or fly 100 qualifying one-way segments) in the calendar year and you get an unlimited companion fare through December 31, 2027. From January 1, 2027, welcome bonuses, referral bonuses, anniversary points and bonus-category points no longer count. According to The Points Guy and Doctor of Credit, the welcome bonus on a Southwest card approved by December 2, 2026 still counts under the 2026 rules even if it posts in early 2027. Approval on December 3 or later still earns the bonus, but it no longer helps with the pass.

Sources: Southwest's "How to earn A-List, A-List Preferred, and Companion Pass (2026)" and "Southwest Companion Pass in 2027" pages, Chase's Southwest card application terms, and coverage by The Points Guy, Doctor of Credit (updated November 25, 2026) and One Mile at a Time. Checked November 26, 2026.

1. The switchover: 2026 versus 2027

Southwest's 2027 page states that Companion Pass qualifying points will be retired after December 31, 2026. From 2027 the pass is earned through Tier Qualifying Points (TQPs) or qualifying flights, with cobranded cards earning 1 TQP per $1 regardless of category.

Southwest's own 2026 page contains two statements that sit uneasily together: one says bonus points earned on Chase Southwest cards do not count toward the Companion Pass; another says card points posted in 2026, including bonus points, do count. The Points Guy and Doctor of Credit both follow the second reading and cite December 2 as the approval deadline under transition rules. We could not find the December 2 date on Southwest's public pages, so it is worth confirming with Southwest before applying.

2. Worked examples: three starting points

Current public offers (per Doctor of Credit and The Points Guy): personal Plus 80,000 points after $1,000 in 3 months ($99 fee), Premier 85,000 after $2,000 ($149), Priority 90,000 after $3,000 ($229); Premier Business 60,000 after $3,000 ($149), Performance Business 80,000 after $5,000 ($299). Base earning is assumed at 1 point per $1.

Scenario B is the personal-plus-business combination described by The Points Guy. The pass is only worth what your companion actually flies: Southwest says companion travel costs only taxes and fees, from $5.60 one-way. Two people taking six round trips in 2027 at about $150 per one-way ticket would save roughly $1,800 in fares, comfortably above the $248 in first-year fees for the two cards in Scenario B. If you fly once or twice a year, the bonus points alone are often worth more than the pass.

3. Eligibility: who can still apply

  • Personal cards are mutually exclusive. Chase's terms say the card is not available to current holders of any Southwest personal card, or to anyone who received a new cardmember bonus on any Southwest personal card in the past 24 months. Plus, Premier and Priority count as one family.
  • Business cards are assessed separately. Per The Points Guy, the business-card restriction applies only to that specific business card, so holding a personal card does not block a business application.
  • 5/24. Doctor of Credit notes these cards are subject to Chase's 5/24 rule.
  • No fee waiver. All three personal cards charge the annual fee in year one.

4. Pro tips

  • Watch your December statement date, not December 31. Southwest counts card points by the date they post to your Rapid Rewards account, not the purchase date. Spending after your December statement closes appears on the January statement and posts in 2027, after qualifying points are retired. The transition rule covers welcome bonuses; top-up points from everyday spending still need to post in 2026.
  • Top up through partners. Partner points (hotels, car rentals, the shopping portal; excluding bonus points on Chase cards) count, also by posting date. Aim to finish by mid-December.
  • Choose your companion carefully. You may change your designated companion up to three times per calendar year; each change takes 21 business days and requires cancelling existing companion reservations first.
  • From 2027, it is about real spending. At 1 TQP per $1, three companion trips require 95,000 TQPs. Infrequent flyers spending less than that will find the pass largely out of reach through cards alone.

5. Common mistakes

  • "A new card gets the 10,000-point boost." The boost goes to members holding a card on the first business day of the calendar year. A card opened in November misses 2026's boost, and the 2027 boost is irrelevant once qualifying points are retired.
  • "The offer runs to December 14, so there is time." Doctor of Credit shows the elevated personal-card offers extended to December 14, but the approval deadline for the transition rule remains December 2.
  • "Submitting the application is enough." Coverage says "approved." If your application goes to pending review, call Chase promptly.
  • "Companion flights earn points." Southwest's terms say no points or qualifying points are awarded for the companion's flights.

FAQ

If I earn the pass in December 2026, how long does it last?

Southwest's 2027 page says a pass earned in 2026 under current rules provides unlimited companion travel through December 31, 2027.

Can I apply with an ITIN instead of an SSN?

The cards are issued by Chase, so ITIN applicants follow Chase's general process, covered in our separate article on applying to Chase with an ITIN. Never enter an ITIN in a field that accepts only an SSN.

I am registered for a Companion Pass promotion. Am I affected?

Southwest says existing registrations will be honored once you meet the promotion's requirements.
