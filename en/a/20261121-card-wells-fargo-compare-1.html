Which Wells Fargo Credit Card? Active Cash vs Autograph vs Autograph Journey vs Reflect

Nov 21, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Wells Fargo's four main cards from the official pages: unlimited 2% on Active Cash, 3X categories on Autograph, 5X hotels on Journey, and 21 months at 0% on Reflect.

Bottom line: for simple cash back, pick Active Cash (2% on everything). If you spend a lot on dining, gas, travel and phone bills, pick Autograph (3X categories, no annual fee). Frequent hotel and airline spenders should look at Autograph Journey ($95 fee). To pay down existing debt or finance a big purchase, look at Reflect (0% for 21 months).

Figures below come from each card's page on Wells Fargo's official site, checked on November 21, 2026. Bonuses change often, and Wells Fargo says offers can differ by channel, so double-check before you apply.

After the intro period the variable APR is 18.74%, 24.74% or 28.74% on Active Cash and Autograph, and 17.74%, 24.24% or 28.49% on Reflect, depending on creditworthiness.

Easy-to-miss benefits

  • Cell phone protection: pay your monthly phone bill with the card and you get coverage against damage or theft—up to $600 on Active Cash, Autograph and Reflect, up to $1,000 on Autograph Journey, with a $25 deductible.
  • Autograph's “travel” is broad: the official list includes airlines, hotels, car rentals, cruise lines, travel agencies, discount travel sites and campgrounds.
  • Autograph and Journey both earn Wells Fargo Rewards points, which can be used together.

Which one fits you

  • One simple card: Active Cash.
  • Lots of dining and gas, no annual fee: Autograph.
  • A few hotel stays and flights a year and you'll use the $50 airline credit: Autograph Journey.
  • High-interest debt to move and pay off: Reflect—just clear it within 21 months.

FAQ

Can I apply for two Wells Fargo cards at once?

Wells Fargo says you may not qualify for another Wells Fargo consumer card if you opened one in the last 4 months. See our companion article on Wells Fargo application rules.

I've seen a $200 Active Cash bonus online—which is right?

Offers vary by channel and over time. This article only reports the $100 / $500 offer shown on the public page on the day we checked.
