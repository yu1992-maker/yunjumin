Does a Wise UK account need a UK address? Opening from abroad

Nov 8, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

You can open Wise GBP details with ID and proof of address from where you live, but it is not a bank account.

You do not need a UK address. If you live in a country Wise supports, including the United States and Canada, you can open Wise with ID and proof of address from where you live, add a GBP balance, and receive a UK account number and sort code. These details are not a traditional UK bank account, as explained below.

What Wise says

  • The Wise help center lists the countries where you can hold a balance, including the UK, the U.S., Canada, Australia, Germany, Singapore and Japan. What matters is where you live, not whether you have a UK address.
  • The help page on GBP account details states no UK address or residence requirement. You add GBP, choose "Get account details", Wise verifies your identity, and the details appear.
  • An article on the Wise U.S. site says you can open an account and get local GBP details before arriving in the UK, and that U.S. identity and address documents can be used.
  • Wise also says this is not a bank account. It is an account number, sort code and BIC for receiving money.

What to prepare

According to the third-party reviewer Exiap, you must be 18 or over, provide ID and proof of address, upload the documents and take a selfie. Wise may ask for more.

  1. Valid ID, such as a passport or driver's license.
  2. Proof of address in your country of residence, such as a utility bill or bank statement. Wise publishes no single list, so follow the app.
  3. A phone or device with a camera.

Steps

  1. Register a personal account on the Wise site or app for your country and verify your identity.
  2. Add a GBP balance.
  3. Choose "Get your account details".
  4. After verification, the account number, sort code and BIC are shown.
  5. Give them to the UK payer, who can pay you by local transfer.

Fees

  • Opening, minimum balance and monthly fee: $0 according to the U.S. article.
  • Conversion at the mid-market rate from 0.43%. The Exiap figure is dated September 2023, so check Wise's fee page.
  • Physical multi-currency card: $9, from the same page.

Where applications stall

  • Unsupported residence. The help page says currencies and details are paused in Thailand, for example, and Nevada residents cannot hold a balance.
  • Mismatched documents. If the address proof and ID disagree, more documents will be requested.
  • Wanting an actual UK bank account. Traditional banks usually need a UK address. Nonresidents are generally directed to international divisions with high minimum deposits, according to third-party guides.

U.S. tax note

U.S. tax residents may have FBAR (FinCEN) obligations for foreign accounts. The general threshold is more than $10,000 in aggregate during the year. Whether a Wise balance is a foreign financial account is a point on which we found only forum discussion, with some on British Expats arguing that an account with a UK sort code may be reportable. Views differ and this is not an official conclusion; ask a CPA.

Frequently asked questions

Without a UK address, do I get a UK bank account?

No. You get UK-format account details for receiving money. Wise itself says it is not a bank account.

Can I use U.S. or Canadian proof of address?

The Wise U.S. article says U.S. identity and address documents work. Requirements elsewhere, including Canada, may differ slightly, so follow the sign-up page.

Can I receive a UK salary?

Technically an employer can pay by local transfer. Whether an employer or public body accepts such an account varies, so ask first. The help page specifically excludes only UK superannuation transfers.
