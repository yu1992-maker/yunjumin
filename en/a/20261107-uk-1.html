When UK Bank Switching Bonuses Are Paid: 10 to 60 Days (2026)

Nov 7, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Payment timelines at Lloyds, Santander, NatWest, HSBC, Nationwide and others, counted from meeting the conditions.

UK switching bonuses have no single payment timeline. According to Which? in November 2026, the fastest pays within 10 days of qualifying (Nationwide), most pay within 14 working days to 30 days, and HSBC can take up to 60. The clock runs from meeting every condition, or from the start of the switch, not from account opening or the first deposit.

Figures come mainly from Which? comparisons of September and November 2026. Terms change often, and disagreements are flagged below. The Current Account Switch Service (CASS) is for people with a UK address and an existing UK account; readers in the US or Canada should confirm eligibility first.

Two separate clocks

  • The qualifying clock: how long you have to complete deposits, direct debits or card payments, commonly 28 to 60 days.
  • The payment clock: how long the bank takes to pay after you qualify.

Many readers watch only the first and expect payment on the day of the deposit. The payment period starts only when all conditions are met.

Where sources disagree

  • Nationwide: the November article says within 10 days of meeting all conditions; September says within 10 days of the switch completing. Current terms govern.
  • First Direct: September says payment on the 20th of the month after qualifying; November is silent. The conditions also differ slightly (September mentions five card payments and 45 days).
  • Santander: the November heading says £200 and the text £240; September also says £240 but gives no payment time, only 60 days to complete the switch. Older £175 and £200 versions were past offers.
  • Lloyds: older pages mention 10 or 3 working days; the current figure is 14.
  • HSBC: a separate Premier offer reportedly pays within 70 days. Do not confuse it with the 60 days above.

Estimating your payment date

  1. List every condition: deposits, direct debits, card payments, app login.
  2. Take the date the last one was completed as your qualifying date.
  3. Add the bank's period: about 10 days for Nationwide, 14 working days from the switch start for Lloyds, 30 days for NatWest and Santander, 60 for HSBC.
  4. Put the date in your calendar and contact the bank only after it passes.

How long the switch itself takes

Which? says the CASS switch date must be at least seven working days after the new account opens and cannot fall on a weekend or public holiday. The new bank moves payments and balance and closes the old account. Where a bonus runs from the start or end of the switch, add those seven or more working days.

What delays or forfeits a bonus

  • Too few direct debits: Lloyds does not count those set up after the switch starts.
  • Closing early: Santander requires both accounts to stay open until payment.
  • Joint accounts or repeat claims: NatWest/RBS exclude joint accounts and switches from within the group.
  • Split deposits: NatWest counts each instalment only after a full 24 hours.
  • Confusing the two clocks: HSBC's 60 days is a payment period after qualifying, not after opening.

If it does not arrive

  1. Check that every condition was met, with screenshots.
  2. Confirm the stated period has passed.
  3. Ask through official channels, giving opening, switch and qualifying dates.
  4. Then complain formally. Which? says that after eight weeks without a reply, or if dissatisfied, you may go to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

FAQ

Is the bonus paid on the day of the deposit?

Generally not. The fastest is within 10 days of qualifying; most take 14 working days to 30 days, HSBC up to 60.

Can I close either account before payment?

Not advisable. Santander requires both to stay open; others do not all say so, but closing early may forfeit the bonus.

Which source should I follow?

The bank's current terms. Third-party articles lag.
