Credit Freezes Beyond the Big Three: NCTUE, Innovis and the One-Hour Rule

Nov 27, 20265 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Innovis, NCTUE and ChexSystems offer free freezes too. Federal law sets a one-business-day freeze and a one-hour online lift.

A security freeze is a free statutory right in the US, but the three nationwide bureaus cover only lending data. Innovis, the telecom and utility database NCTUE, and the checking-account database ChexSystems are separate and must be frozen one by one. Under 15 U.S.C. §1681c-1, a nationwide bureau must place a freeze within one business day of an online or phone request, lift it within one hour, and allow a temporary lift for a period you specify. A freeze has no effect on your credit score and does not stop existing creditors from seeing your file.

Sources: CFPB, “What is a credit freeze or security freeze on my credit report?” (updated 17 September 2025); the text of FCRA §1681c-1 and the 2018 amendment notes for Pub. L. 115-174; the freeze pages of Innovis, NCTUE and ChexSystems. Checked on 27 November 2026.

1. The statutory clock: one business day, five business days, one hour

Section 301 of Pub. L. 115-174, effective 24 May 2018, added subsections (i) and (j) to §1681c-1 and replaced a patchwork of state fees with a nationwide free freeze.

These deadlines bind the nationwide agencies defined in §1681a(p): Equifax, Experian and TransUnion. Innovis, NCTUE and ChexSystems also offer free freezes, but their procedures are set out on their own sites.

NCTUE states that a freeze makes your file unavailable for opening new accounts. That matters because a common form of identity theft is a phone contract or utility account opened in your name, and those checks often run against telecom and utility data rather than the big three. A ChexSystems freeze targets fraudulent checking accounts. The CFPB's List of Consumer Reporting Companies names further specialty agencies.

3. Scenario: a year-end data breach

In late November 2026, an H-1B holder receives a retailer's breach notice: name, address and date of birth may be exposed. They plan to apply for a new card in January 2027.

  1. On 27 November, freeze Equifax, Experian and TransUnion online. By law the freezes take effect by the next business day, with written confirmation within five business days.
  2. The same day, freeze Innovis, NCTUE and ChexSystems, and keep the Innovis 10-digit PIN and the ChexSystems PIN separately.
  3. Before applying in January, set a short temporary lift at all three bureaus, say 10 to 13 January, since issuers do not always disclose which bureau they pull. An online lift takes effect within an hour and the freeze returns automatically.
  4. If identity theft is confirmed, file at IdentityTheft.gov and request a seven-year extended fraud alert.

The statutory cost of all this is zero. The CFPB notes that the “credit locks” bureaus sell are often fee-based, bundled with other paid services, and no more effective than a free freeze.

4. Pro tips

  • Use a dated lift, not a removal. The CFPB confirms you can lift a freeze free of charge for a period you specify, on the same timelines as a full removal. An end date means you cannot forget to refreeze.
  • Protected-consumer records for children. §1681c-1(j) covers children under 16. If no file exists, the bureau creates one at a parent's request and freezes it; that record cannot be used for credit. Immigrant families can do this once a child has an SSN.
  • Freeze telecom data before you need a phone contract. NCTUE lifts are free; open the file for the contract, then refreeze.
  • Prescreened offers are a separate opt-out. A freeze does not remove you from prescreened mailing lists. Under §1681c-1(b), an extended alert backed by an identity theft report excludes you from those lists for five years; Innovis also runs its own opt-out page.

5. Common misconceptions

  • “A freeze lowers my score.” The CFPB says it has no effect on credit scores whatsoever.
  • “A freeze protects my existing cards.” Existing creditors can still access your file and existing accounts work as normal. Fraud on an existing card is a billing dispute.
  • “One bureau is enough.” The CFPB says to contact each bureau separately, and specialty agencies need separate requests too.
  • “Employers, landlords and insurers can't see a frozen file.” The CFPB notes that the federal free-freeze rule does not apply to reports requested for employment, tenant screening or insurance.
  • “A lock is a freeze by another name.” A lock is a commercial product governed by contract; a freeze's timelines and zero cost are set by federal law.

FAQ

Can I freeze with an ITIN and no SSN?

Online identity checks and mail forms generally default to an SSN; NCTUE's mail instructions, for example, ask for one. ITIN holders should call the agency first to confirm acceptable documents, then use mail.

How soon after lifting can I apply?

For the nationwide bureaus, an online lift must be completed within one hour. To be safe, set the window to start the day before you apply.

What about an application already in review?

If the issuer pulls your report after the freeze takes effect, the application may be held or declined. Keep the lift window open through any follow-up review.

Verify details on each agency's official site. For legal questions about identity theft, consult an attorney or a local legal aid organisation.
