How Many Working Days Does a UK CASS Switch Take?

Nov 7, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

A full CASS switch usually takes seven working days; bonus timing depends on each bank. Steps, timeline and pitfalls.

A full switch through the Current Account Switch Service (CASS) usually takes seven working days: the switch date must be at least seven working days after the new account opens and cannot be a weekend or bank holiday. When the bonus is paid is a matter for each bank, from about 7 to 10 days to a month or more.

What CASS is and why bonuses require it

CASS is the UK's free official switching service, with more than 50 banks and building societies. You open a new account and request a switch; the new bank moves the balance, Direct Debits, standing orders and incoming payments, and the old account is closed. Most bonuses require this full switch, according to both MoneySavingExpert and Which?.

Timeline

  1. Day 0: open the new account, request the switch, agree a date, and sign the switch agreement and closure instruction, which the new bank handles.
  2. Day 0 to switch date: at least seven working days. Keep using the old account.
  3. Switch date: balance and payment arrangements move; the old account closes.
  4. Afterwards: payments sent to the old account are redirected, for at least three years according to MoneySavingExpert.
  5. Bonus: see the bank's terms.
Weekends and bank holidays do not count, so the calendar time may be longer.

Checklist before switching

  • Read the bonus terms. Common conditions are CASS, at least two Direct Debits or standing orders, a minimum deposit and time limits.
  • Download several years of statements. They are hard to obtain after closure.
  • Set up no new Direct Debits in the seven working days before the switch. Officially, these will not transfer.
  • Agree any overdraft with the new bank first, or arrange repayment with the old one.
  • Joint accounts can switch only to a joint account held by the same people (Which?).
  • Recurring card payments are outside CASS. Update subscriptions yourself.
  • Avoid heavy payment days. Which? advises against switching on the day most Direct Debits leave.
  • If a mortgage or car loan is pending, wait. Which? warns that several credit searches in a short period may matter.

Full and partial switches compared

Old accountClosedKept
Switch guaranteeAppliesDoes not apply

Under the guarantee, if an error in the switch causes payment problems, the new bank must correct it and refund the resulting charges and interest. A partial switch has no such cover.

When bonuses are paid: sources differ

  • MoneySavingExpert says some banks pay about 7 to 10 days after the switch, others a month or more.
  • Another search result says one Lloyds offer paid 45 to 90 working days after completion. It is an aggregator and we did not verify the bank's wording.

The gap is wide. Follow the current terms of the bank you choose and keep a screenshot.

Tax

MoneySavingExpert says switching bonuses are usually tax-free, which is the UK position. If you have filing obligations in the US or Canada, check separately; we found no official statement.

FAQ

Can CASS be faster than seven working days?

The official page and Which? both say the date must be at least seven working days ahead, with no mention of an express option.

What about payments still sent to the old account?

They are redirected, for at least three years according to MoneySavingExpert. Tell payers anyway.

Can a partial switch earn a bonus?

Most of the best bonuses require a full switch. A few banks may be more flexible; read the terms.
