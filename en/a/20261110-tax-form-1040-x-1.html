Found a mistake after filing? How to complete and submit Form 1040-X

Nov 10, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Correcting a filed return with Form 1040-X: when to amend, deadlines, columns A/B/C, e-file versus mail, tracking and state returns.

Correct a filed return with Form 1040-X (Amended U.S. Individual Income Tax Return) rather than refiling the whole return. A refund claim is generally due within 3 years of filing the original return or 2 years of paying the tax, whichever is later. Amended returns can now be e-filed through tax software or mailed on paper. The following follows the official IRS instructions.

Do you need to amend?

  • Amend if your filing status, income, deductions, credits, dependents or tax liability change (the IRS's own list).
  • No need if the IRS has already corrected the error, or accepted your return without a form you forgot that was not required.
  • Only seeking abatement of penalties or interest: the 1040-X instructions direct you to Form 843.
  • IRS note from May 2026: claiming the "no tax on tips" deduction may require an amended return.

Deadlines

The IRS forms page also notes extensions for 2020 and 2021 amended returns under the Federal Disaster Tax Relief Act of 2023, without detail; check the official guidance.

Completing the form

  1. Have the original return and the corrected figures ready. Up to three amended returns may be filed for the same year (the e-file limit is also three).
  2. Fill columns A, B and C:
    • Column A: amounts from the original return, or as previously adjusted by you or the IRS.
    • Column B: the net increase or decrease for each line.
    • Column C: the corrected amount, column A plus or minus column B. Lines that did not change repeat column A.
  3. Part II, Explanation of Changes: state what changed and why.
  4. Attach every new or changed form and schedule. The instructions warn that returns missing relevant forms will be sent back. The IRS also asks for a complete corrected Form 1040, 1040-SR or 1040-NR.
  5. Sign: paper returns require a handwritten signature; electronic or typed signatures are not accepted. A joint filer's spouse must also sign. E-filed amendments use a PIN.

  • A paid preparer e-filing on your behalf needs Form 8879, a new one for each amended submission.
  • If the original current-year return was filed on paper and you are amending a prior year, use paper as well.
  • Refunds can be split with Form 8888 but cannot buy savings bonds.

Where to mail it

  • Florida residents: Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service, Austin, TX 73301-0052 (December 2025 instructions).
  • Foreign addresses, APO/FPO, filers of Form 2555 or 4563, or dual-status aliens: Austin, TX 73301-0215.
  • Other states: see the IRS Where to File page. If you received an IRS notice, use the address on the notice.

Paying additional tax and interest

  • If you owe more, include Form 1040-V and make the check payable to "United States Treasury".
  • Paying by the April filing deadline avoids penalties and interest.
  • If filing after the deadline, do not calculate interest or penalties yourself; the IRS will adjust them.
  • Interest runs from the tax due date even if you had an extension.
  • An excessive refund claim can attract a penalty of 20% of the disallowed amount unless there is reasonable cause.

Tracking progress

  • Use "Where's My Amended Return?" online or by phone at 866-464-2050. Status appears 3 weeks after submission and covers the current year and three prior years.
  • Three statuses: Received, Adjusted (account changed) and Completed (details mailed).
  • Not covered: carrybacks, injured spouse claims, business amended returns and amended returns with foreign addresses.
  • Returns that are incomplete, unsigned, involve identity theft or need specialised review may take more than 12 weeks.

Do not forget the state return

A federal change may require a state amendment. The IRS says to contact your state tax agency and not to attach a state amended return to the federal one. Florida has no personal income tax, so residents generally have no state amendment to file; other states have their own rules.

FAQ

My original refund has not arrived. Can I amend now?

The IRS pages we checked do not say you must wait. The instructions say Form 1040-X should be filed only after the original return. If unsure, call the IRS or ask your preparer.

Can I amend again if the first amendment was wrong?

Yes, up to three amended returns per year according to the IRS e-file page; the figures from the previous amendment become the new column A.

Can I file it directly on the IRS website?

No. Electronic filing is only through tax software; older years require paper, as shown in the table.

Rely on the latest IRS instructions. For multiple years, foreign income or accounts, IRS notices or audits, consult a CPA or attorney.
