Chase Total Checking $400 bonus: direct deposit rules and timing

Nov 8, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Deposit $1,000 within 90 days; terms say payment within 15 days. Cases, expiry dates and a checklist of pitfalls.

The new-account bonus on Chase Total Checking is $400 for direct deposits totaling $1,000 within 90 days. The terms quoted by Bankrate and BankBonus say it is paid within 15 days of qualifying. Users report everything from a few days to much longer.

1. Core terms

Sources differ on when the 90 days begin: Doctor of Credit says account opening, Bankrate says coupon enrollment. The safe course is to collect the coupon on Chase's website, open the account and complete the deposit early.

2. What counts as direct deposit

  • Counts: paychecks, pensions and government benefits such as Social Security.
  • Does not count: person-to-person transfers such as Zelle, per terms quoted in Doctor of Credit comments.
  • Disputed: ACH transfers from your own account elsewhere. Some BankBonus users say theirs did not count, which is unverified. The pages above are silent, so do not rely on it.

Without a job, ask Chase or consult the official list of qualifying deposits linked by Doctor of Credit before deciding.

3. Steps

  1. Check eligibility. You must not be a current Chase checking customer. Accounts closed within the past 90 days disqualify you, as does closure for a negative balance within three years (Bankrate). You must not have had a Chase checking bonus in two years.
  2. Collect the coupon, then open, online or at a branch.
  3. Opening deposit. Doctor of Credit says up to $50 can be funded online by debit card (no prepaid cards); branches do not accept debit or credit card funding.
  4. Redirect your paycheck. Give your employer's HR system the new routing and account numbers. It usually takes effect from the next pay cycle, so do not wait until day 80.
  5. Reach $1,000 in one or several deposits.
  6. Keep screenshots of the opening date and each deposit in case you need to query.
  7. Keep the account at least six months. BankBonus's terms say Chase takes the bonus back if you close sooner.

4. How long it takes

The terms say 15 days. These forum reports are for reference only and may not reflect current practice:

  • A Doctor of Credit commenter received a checking bonus about two business days after the deposit.
  • In a 2021 case with a $200 bonus, a deposit on April 2 was followed by the bonus on April 13, about 11 days.
  • Some say Chase often pays on Tuesdays, so allow a week.
  • A few waited more than 90 days and had to call or send a secure message, mostly for savings or combined bonuses.

Earlier promotions said 10 business days after qualifying. Do not confuse that with the current 15 days.

5. Monthly fee and waivers

Sources differ on the fee: Doctor of Credit's summary says $12 and its fee section $15; Chase's product page also mentions $15. Follow Chase's fee schedule at opening. Waivers, per Doctor of Credit and BankBonus:

  • Qualifying electronic deposits of $500 or more a month;
  • A minimum daily balance of $1,500;
  • An average daily balance of $5,000 across qualifying Chase accounts (BankBonus also mentions linked checking).

Doctor of Credit says there is no fee for the first two statement cycles. The direct deposit itself satisfies the $500 test, but keep it up after the bonus arrives.

6. When the offer ends

Sources differ:

  • Bankrate's page says April 15, 2026.
  • Doctor of Credit's summary still says April 15, 2026; its update log records repeated extensions, most recently to January 27, 2027, so the page is inconsistent.
  • BankBonus, updated October 10, 2026, gives January 27, 2027.

As of November 2026 the offer is probably still available, but Chase can change it at any time. Rely on the coupon page.

7. Other points

  • Taxable. Doctor of Credit says the bonus is taxable income. Expect a 1099 next year if the amount is large enough.
  • Referrals. Doctor of Credit says the person referred gets the standard $400 and the referrer $50.
  • A larger option. A $900 combined checking and savings bonus requires $15,000 held for 90 days; Doctor of Credit considers it better value if you can meet it.

Questions

Can I meet it with a single $1,000 direct deposit?

Yes. The requirement is $1,000 or more in total, in one deposit or several, as long as the type qualifies.

Fifteen days have passed and nothing arrived. What now?

Confirm that the coupon was collected before opening and that Chase recognized the deposit, then contact Chase with the records. Some users were paid only after asking.

Can I close the account right after the bonus?

Not advisable. Terms quoted by BankBonus say closing within six months forfeits the bonus, and you cannot earn another Chase checking bonus for two years.
