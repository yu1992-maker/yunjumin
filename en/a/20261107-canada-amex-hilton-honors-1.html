Amex Canada Hilton Honors card: welcome bonus and annual fee

Nov 7, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Launched in Canada on October 29, 2026: $150 annual fee, up to 70,000 points plus a free night, $3,000 spend in 3 months, offer ends January 11, 2027.

The Amex Canada Hilton Honors card carries a $150 annual fee, with free additional cards. The welcome offer is up to 70,000 points plus a Free Night Reward: 60,000 points and the free night after $3,000 in purchases in the first three months, plus 10,000 points for $500 spent in month 13. Apply by January 11, 2027.

This is Hilton's first cobranded credit card in Canada, launched on October 29, 2026 according to Prince of Travel and MoneySense. The details below come from their reporting, not Amex's own wording; check Amex Canada's site before applying.

Earning the welcome bonus

StageRequirementReward
First 3 months$3,000 in purchases60,000 Hilton Honors points + Free Night Reward
Month 13$500 in purchases that month10,000 more points
TotalUp to 70,000 points + 1 free night
  • The second tier arrives in month 13, so do not cancel within the first year.
  • The $3,000 spend also earns regular points at the rates below.
  • Current or former holders of this card are not eligible for the welcome bonus (Prince of Travel).

Free Night Reward conditions

According to Prince of Travel:

  • Valid for a standard room at Hilton properties, double occupancy;
  • Amex's terms set no points cap, though some hotels are excluded;
  • Must be booked by phone;
  • Valid for 12 months from issue.

MoneySense does not state the free night's value, validity or eligible hotels and refers readers to Amex. The official terms govern.

Per MoneySense's fee table, balance transfers are not offered and instalment plans cost up to 1.2% per month.

Everyday earn rates

  • Hilton hotel spending (including dining and spa charged to the room): 6 points per $1;
  • Restaurants, coffee shops, bars, grocery, gas and food delivery in Canada: 4 points per $1 (MoneySense lists only gas, grocery and restaurants);
  • Everything else: 2 points per $1.

At these rates, the $3,000 minimum spend earns at least 6,000 extra points at 2x, or 12,000 at 4x. This is our estimate from the stated rates; actual postings govern.

Gold status and spending rewards

  1. Hilton Honors Gold: automatic with the card. Benefits include a base-points bonus on stays (Prince of Travel cites 80%), space-available upgrades up to executive floors, a daily food and beverage credit or continental breakfast depending on brand and region, digital check-in and free Wi-Fi.
  2. Annual spend reward: 50,000 points after $12,000 in purchases in a calendar year, earnable every year.
  3. Diamond status: after $40,000 in calendar-year purchases, valid for that year and the next.
  4. Fifth night free: on standard-room award stays booked entirely with points.

Is the $150 fee worth it? A method

  1. Total the first-year points: 60,000 + 10,000 + everyday earnings.
  2. Value them using your own redemptions. We found no reliable official valuation, and media estimates vary, so use the points actually required at hotels you book.
  3. Subtract the $150 fee and the 2.5% foreign transaction fee. Frequent spenders abroad should look elsewhere.
  4. After renewal there is no welcome bonus; only everyday earning, Gold status and the $12,000 annual reward remain.

FAQ

Do additional cards cost anything?

No; both outlets say additional cards are free. Their spending counts toward the $12,000 and $40,000 thresholds (Prince of Travel).

When does the welcome offer end?

Both outlets say applications close January 11, 2027. Some search results mention January 13, but that date relates to the US Hilton cards; rely on Amex Canada's site.

Can I earn the bonus again if I have held this card?

No. Prince of Travel says current and former holders are ineligible.
