Moving Amex Canada Points to a US Account: Cross-Border Transfer Steps and Caveats

Nov 11, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Amex Canada Membership Rewards can move to a US account through an international transfer, but reportedly only once every 12 months, at an exchange rate, and by phone.

It can be done, but not with a click online. According to Prince of Travel, if you hold Amex accounts in your own name in both Canada and the US, you can call to request an "International Transfer." Amex does not publish this process and the rules may have changed, so confirm with Amex before acting.

Note: details on ratios and frequency limits come mainly from a March 2024 Prince of Travel article quoting Amex chat agents at the time. Amex has not published them and they may have changed.

The usual order is to open a US card through Global Transfer first, then consider moving points.

Step one: get a US Amex account

Frugal Flyer (updated November 2025) lists common Global Transfer requirements:

  • an Amex credit card in good standing held for at least about three months (longer is better)
  • a US address (your own, a school's or a friend's; readers reported in September 2025 that phone agents rejected a mailbox address and asked for a physical one)
  • a US bank account (Amex may request a statement, and you need it to pay in dollars)
  • a passport, or an SSN or ITIN

Online, enter the US address, tick "no US credit history but have a card or loan in a listed country," authorize with your Canadian Amex login, choose no SSN, enter your passport and annual income. The result may be instant approval, pending or decline; if pending, call, as you may only need to supply an ID photo or bank statement. You can also apply by phone through the Global Card Transfer team at 1-877-621-2639 (per Frugal Flyer), which usually shows as pending first.

Global Transfer generally applies only to the first card; after that you are assessed on your US file. Whether business cards qualify is unclear.

Step two: transferring points (per Prince of Travel)

  1. Have both card numbers ready: the sending account and the receiving account.
  2. Call Amex in the sending country (Canada, for Canada to US).
  3. State the amount, minimum 1,000 points.
  4. Allow one to two weeks.

Sources differ: Prince of Travel says either side used to work but the sending country must now initiate; a user on the same page said a US agent told them the receiving country should. Try one side, then the other, and note agent IDs. The article adds that chat requests do not always succeed and phone is more reliable.

Ratio, frequency and fees

US → Canada1:1
  • Frequency: one international transfer per 12 months.
  • Fees: the Prince of Travel article does not mention any; ask Amex.
  • Example: at C$1.35 per US$1, 75,000 ANA miles would require about 101,250 Canadian MR points. Recalculate at today's rate.

When it is worth doing

The same article says US MR has 13 US-only partners (such as ANA, Virgin Atlantic, Singapore KrisFlyer, Emirates Skywards, Qantas and Iberia), while Canada has none exclusive. The US also tends to see more frequent transfer bonuses. The reverse direction usually adds no partners, with the exception of Marriott Bonvoy, which transferred at 1:1.2 in Canada versus 1:1 in the US (2024 data, may have changed).

With only one transfer a year, lock in a specific redemption first and check whether "converted Canadian points plus US-side miles required" actually beats using Canadian partners such as Aeroplan directly.

Checklist

  • Both accounts must be in the same name; Milesopedia notes that combining points within Canada also requires all cards to be yours.
  • Transfers to airline or hotel partners must go to accounts in your own name (Wealthnorth, citing Amex terms).
  • Verify the US card number and cardholder details before transferring.
  • Confirm the points have posted before moving them to a partner; partner transfers are usually irreversible, subject to each partner's terms.
  • Review the Amex Canada Membership Rewards terms (June 2026 version) on points.

FAQ

Q1: Do my Canadian points move automatically with Global Transfer?

No. Per a Frugal Flyer reader Q&A, they stay in the Canadian account; request an international transfer separately.

Q2: Can I move points back and forth?

Per Prince of Travel, only one international transfer is allowed in 12 months. Confirm with Amex whether this has changed.

Q3: Which country should I call?

Prince of Travel says the sending country; one user was told by a US agent to have the receiving country initiate. Sources differ; start with the sending country and switch if needed.
