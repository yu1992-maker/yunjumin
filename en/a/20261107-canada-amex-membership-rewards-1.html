Amex Canada: what happens to Membership Rewards points when you cancel or downgrade

Nov 7, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

How Membership Rewards points are treated when an Amex Canada card is cancelled or downgraded: conditions for keeping them, transfer and redemption steps, and reinstatement.

In Canada, cancelling your last American Express card that earns Membership Rewards (MR) points can forfeit the points immediately. If another MR card stays open and in good standing, the points can be kept. The safest approach is to redeem or transfer the points before cancelling, or have them combined into another MR card's account first. We found no clear official Canadian statement on whether a downgrade (product change) preserves points, so confirm by phone.

Note: we could not read the full text of Amex Canada's terms PDF. The official wording below comes from Amex's MR page and search-result excerpts. Check the latest terms before relying on exact language.

What Amex says

  • The Amex Canada MR page lists cancelling your card as one of the ways points can be lost.
  • The same page says you can ask Amex to reinstate lost points. Search excerpts indicate the request must be made within three months of the statement showing the forfeiture, the card must be in good standing and a reinstatement fee applies. We could not confirm the fee amount, so we do not cite one.
  • Excerpts from Amex Bank of Canada's terms say, in substance, that cancelling your card forfeits all points immediately, but points are retained if at least one other linked card remains open and in good standing.

These are excerpts, not a full reading. Rely on the latest terms (Amex lists June and August 2026 versions).

Steps before cancelling

  1. Sign in, note your current balance and take a screenshot.
  2. List which Amex cards earn MR (confirm whether personal and business cards share one points account).
  3. If you have another MR card, call or use secure messaging to have points combined into it. Prince of Travel says calling is usually faster and can handle the merge and cancellation together.
  4. If not, redeem for travel or gift cards, or transfer to airline or hotel partners. Check the current partner list and ratios first; for example, search results indicate Etihad Guest ceased to be a Canadian MR transfer partner on June 20, 2026.
  5. Confirm the points have left the account before cancelling.
  6. Keep call notes, reference numbers or confirmations.

Where sources differ

  • 30-day grace period: The Points Guy quotes US Amex language allowing 30 days to use points after cancellation (provided you keep another card). We did not see equivalent Canadian wording. Assume it does not exist.
  • Other banks: Prince of Travel notes 60 to 90 days of grace at banks such as RBC and TD, but says points in proprietary programs like Amex's are forfeited on cancellation unless transferred or used beforehand.
  • Credit Card Genius: says points are usually lost on cancellation; for Amex Cobalt it suggests points might move to a new account when switching to another Amex card. That is a possibility, not a guarantee.

Downgrade considerations

  • US blogs such as Milestalk say MR points sit in one master account and survive as long as one MR card stays open, with product changes made within the same family. That is the US position and may not apply in Canada.
  • One site claiming to be a "2026 downgrade guide" says downgrades keep all points; we consider it unreliable.
  • When downgrading by phone, ask four things: whether points are kept; when the annual fee changes and whether any is refunded; when the old card's insurance and lounge benefits end (Prince of Travel notes cancelling Platinum ends Priority Pass immediately); and whether you can later earn a welcome bonus on the new card.
  • The US blog Rene's Points says phone downgrades usually do not earn the lower card's welcome bonus. We found nothing confirming the Canadian position.
  • Frequent Miler has recorded US cardholders receiving a downgrade incentive (such as 25,000 MR points) and others receiving nothing, so outcomes vary.

If points have already been forfeited

  1. Contact Amex promptly. The official page sets a three-month limit, measured from the statement showing the forfeiture.
  2. Explain the situation, request reinstatement and ask the fee.
  3. Reinstatement is discretionary; Amex's terms say points are reinstated only if it agrees.

FAQ

Is there a grace period to move points after cancelling?

We found no clear Canadian official grace period. US sources mention 30 days, but that cannot be confirmed for Canada. Transfer or redeem before cancelling.

Can I transfer points to family members?

Credit Card Genius says points cannot be moved to another person's account. You may be able to redeem for transferable items; check current terms first.

I cancelled and lost my points. Is there any recourse?

You can request reinstatement, but the official page sets a time limit and fee, and approval is at Amex's discretion. Act quickly.

Note: rely on Amex Canada's latest terms and customer service. For business cards, deceased cardholders or large balances, consult a licensed professional.
