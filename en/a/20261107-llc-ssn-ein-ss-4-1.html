Getting an EIN Without an SSN: Form SS-4 by Fax, Mail or Phone

Nov 7, 20264 min readYunjumin Forum editors

Without an SSN or ITIN you cannot apply for an EIN online. How to complete Form SS-4, where to fax or mail it, phone applications, timing and common rejections.

Foreign individuals without an SSN or ITIN cannot use the IRS online EIN tool. They must file Form SS-4 by fax, by mail or, if the principal place of business is outside the U.S., by phone. Fax is fastest: the IRS says about four business days, versus about four weeks by mail.

Why the online route is closed

The IRS online application requires the responsible party to have an SSN or ITIN, and is available only to applicants whose principal business is in the U.S. or its territories. Anyone based abroad, or whose responsible party lacks an SSN or ITIN, must use paper or the phone.

The IRS also limits issuance to one EIN per responsible party per day, whatever the channel, so several companies must be spread across several days. Nominees may not apply; the form must list the true responsible party.

The number is 267-941-1099 (not toll-free), Monday to Friday, 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastern. To check on a mailed application, call 800-829-4933.

Where to fax or mail

It depends on whether the applicant's legal residence, principal place of business or principal office is in one of the 50 states or D.C.

SituationFaxMail
Located in the 50 states or D.C.855-641-6935See the SS-4 instructions
Not in any state (abroad or a U.S. territory)From within the U.S. 855-215-1627; from abroad 304-707-9471Internal Revenue Service, Attn: EIN International Operation, Cincinnati, OH 45999

The fax lines run 24 hours. Include your own return fax number so the IRS can fax the EIN back. International call charges are yours.

Views differ: if an LLC uses only a U.S. registered agent or virtual address while management is abroad, it is unclear whether it is "in a state". The IRS divides by principal place of business but gives no virtual-address example. If unsure, call the international line at 267-941-1099.

Key SS-4 lines for foreign applicants

Based on the SS-4 instructions (December 2025 revision).

  1. Line 7b (responsible party's SSN/ITIN): if the responsible party has none and is not eligible, enter "foreign" or "N/A". Do not leave it blank. Some service providers advise leaving it empty; the IRS instructions say otherwise.
  2. Line 9a (entity type): a foreign entity applying to be a disregarded entity checks "Other" and writes "foreign disregarded entity". A foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity (such as a single-member LLC) that must file Form 5472 checks "Other" and writes "Foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity-Form 5472".
  3. Line 10 (reason for applying): check exactly one; N/A is not accepted.
  4. Line 11 (start date): foreign applicants enter the date business began or was acquired in the U.S.
  5. Lines 16 and 17 (business activity): check one; if none fits, check "Other" and describe, with more detail on line 17.
  6. Signature: individuals sign personally; for foreign applicants any duly authorized person, such as a division manager, may sign. Unsigned forms are returned.

Third-party designee

An attorney, registered agent or accountant can be named as third-party designee, but only with your signed authorization. The authority ends once the EIN is issued, and the EIN notice still goes to the entity. If the designee's address or phone matches the taxpayer's, only fax or mail may be used.

Fax checklist

  1. Download the current SS-4 and instructions; print and complete legibly.
  2. Match the LLC name exactly to the state filing.
  3. Check that lines 7b, 9a, 10 and the signature are complete.
  4. Write a return fax number on the form.
  5. Use the correct fax number from the table and keep the transmission confirmation.
  6. If nothing arrives within about four business days, call; follow the IRS's answer on whether to mail the original.

Common reasons for rejection

  • Missing signature.
  • Lines 7b or 10 left blank or filled incorrectly.
  • LLC name not matching state records.
  • Wrong fax number or mailing address.
  • No return fax number.

FAQ

Can someone else apply using their SSN?

Not advisable. The IRS requires the true responsible party and expressly rejects nominee applications. Using another person's SSN creates problems for later filings and bank accounts.

What comes after the EIN?

Foreign-owned single-member LLCs generally need to consider Form 5472, plus bank accounts and state annual filings. Obligations vary; consult a CPA.

From abroad, phone or fax?

Phone is open only to international applicants and resolves on the call; fax is open to everyone and leaves a paper trail. Without a U.S. phone, an international or online fax service works if the IRS can reach the number you provide.
