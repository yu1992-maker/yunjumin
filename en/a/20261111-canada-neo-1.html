Neo Secured Card Deposit: How to Fund It and How to Get It Back (Canada)

Nov 11, 20263 min readYunjumin Forum editors

The Secured Neo Mastercard needs at least $50 in security funds via e-Transfer or a linked account; it is refundable after you pay off and close the card, with Neo citing about 5 business days.

To fund the Secured Neo Mastercard, you deposit at least $50 into your Neo account by Interac e-Transfer or a linked bank account; that money becomes your "security funds." The deposit is refundable: once the full balance is paid and the card closed, it can be moved to your Neo Everyday account or an external bank account. Until then it stays locked and cannot be withdrawn or used to pay the card.

What the deposit is and how to fund it

  1. Open a Neo Everyday account.
  2. Apply for the secured card and choose how much to deposit. Neo's marketing page says from $50, by e-Transfer or a linked bank account.
  3. The deposit sets your credit limit. Finder gives a range of $50–$10,000 and says the World Elite secured card requires at least $200.
  4. Use the card and pay each month from your Neo account or an external account.
Note: Neo's support page says the security funds are not used to pay your balance. You still have to pay on time.

Limit and deposit are not always equal

A Neo community reply says Flexible Credit secured cards are not necessarily capped by the deposit: a $100 deposit could come with a $500 limit. Neo may also raise the limit without more deposit, with the first opportunity after about three months depending on your profile and payment history. The card remains secured after an increase.

Sources differ: Neo's blog says you may be moved to an unsecured card with the deposit returned once your credit improves, while a community reply says secured cards cannot yet be upgraded directly and the feature is in development. Follow what the app shows you.

Steps to get it back

  1. Pay off the entire balance, including unbilled transactions.
  2. Follow the "close credit card" flow in the app or on the web.
  3. Note that closing disables autopay and declines any further transactions on the card.
  4. Choose to move the security funds to Neo Everyday or an external bank account.
  5. Wait for the funds to arrive.

How long it takes

Neo's official pages give no timeframe. In April 2025 a Neo staff member told a community user that funds usually arrive in about 5 business days after Neo initiates the transfer. In the same thread, another user said their $50 was still in the account after closing in February 2024, so delays do happen. Other threads about unreturned deposits exist (we saw titles but did not verify content). If nothing happens after a week, contact Neo through in-app chat.

Before you close the card

  • Fees: Hardbacon says there is no monthly or annual fee. Finder lists $119.88 a year and says the secured card requires a Build or Grow membership plan. Sources differ; check what your plan actually charges.
  • Interest: Finder lists 19.99%–29.99% on purchases and 22.99%–31.99% on cash advances, set by Neo at sign-up.
  • Reapplying: a community reply says you can reapply after holding the card for six months, which opens a new credit account.
  • Credit history: closing deactivates the account; weigh the effect on the length of your history.

FAQ

Can I withdraw part of the deposit?

The official pages we found mention transfers only after paying off and closing; partial withdrawals are not mentioned. Ask in-app support.

What are the minimum and maximum deposits?

Minimum $50 ($200 for World Elite, per Finder). Finder cites a $10,000 maximum, but Neo's official pages give no clear cap; follow the app.

Where does the refund go?

Neo's support page says to your Neo Everyday account or an external bank account. The options shown when you close the card govern.
