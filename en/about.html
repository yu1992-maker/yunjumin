About Yunjumin Forum

Yunjumin Forum (云居民论坛) publishes practical guides for people building a financial life in the US, the UK and Canada, whether you just arrived or have lived there for years.

  • Credit cards and banking: ITIN and no-SSN options, application rules, sign-up bonus fine print, newcomer packages.
  • Tax: US, UK and Canadian filing deadlines, residency rules, foreign income reporting.
  • Newcomers: building credit from zero, documents you need, common mistakes.

How we write

Every article starts from official pages (banks, IRS, GOV.UK/HMRC, CRA, FCAC, CFPB), adds community data points where useful, and lists all sources. Where sources disagree, we say so.

We never charge fees, act as an agent, or ask for your ID or account details. Anyone claiming to represent us and asking for money is a scammer. Nothing here is tax, legal or financial advice.
